Your plan is in Workspace settings, **Plan & billing**. Owners and Admins can open it. Prices are on the pricing page and are not repeated here.

## What each plan includes

| | Starter | Growth | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| People (seats) | Up to 10 | Up to 50 | Up to 200 |
| SOPs, drafts included | 3 | 20 | 100 |
| AI questions, in total | 50 | 500 | 2,000 |
| Talk and Tables | Not included | Included | Included |
| AI teammates of your own, per person | 3 | 20 | 50 |
| AI teammates shared by the workspace | 3 | 30 | 100 |

Starter is free. A workspace on the Enterprise plan has no limits on these.

## How seats are counted

The **Seats** meter counts people who can sign in, plus open invitations. Deactivated people and expired invitations do not count.

Every way into the workspace takes a seat: an invitation from Members, an invitation an AI teammate prepared, and an invitation sent through the API. When the workspace is full, a new invitation is refused, and someone accepting an older one is told the workspace has no free seat for them right now.

People who work here through another workspace's account are listed under the Seats meter as **In through another workspace**. Each holds a seat here. To free one of those seats, email billing@workwrk.com.

## How AI questions are counted

**AI questions used, in total** is a total for the workspace, not a monthly allowance. These count:

- each Ask AI message,
- each AI teammate message, routine run, group chat answer, answer in Talk, automation step and ask of another teammate (see [Limits and plans for AI teammates](/using/ai-teammates/limits)),
- the AI actions people start in docs, files, forms, tables, canvases, SOPs, KRAs, meetings and the app builder.

A request that fails before the AI answers is not counted. Suggestions nobody asked for, such as field suggestions and goal assessments, never count, run up to a daily total, and stop once the workspace's total is used. Fill with AI has its own daily limit.

On Starter, each person also has 50 free questions in total, counted across every free workspace they use.

## When a limit is reached

A workspace that goes over a limit keeps everyone and everything it has. It can't add more people, or ask the AI, until the plan changes. An AI teammate's routines pause with the reason, and each person resumes theirs after the plan changes.

Talk and Tables are included from Growth. On Starter, turning either on in Workspace settings, **Apps & modules**, says it is included from the Growth plan. A workspace that already had a module on keeps it.

## Change the plan

What **Plan & billing** offers depends on how your workspace pays:

- **Upgrade to Growth** opens checkout, where you choose the seats, when card payment is available for your workspace.
- **Manage billing** opens the billing portal, where you update the card, see invoices or cancel. To change the seats or the plan, email billing@workwrk.com.
- Otherwise the page says **Billing is handled by our team**, with the address to email.

### AppSumo codes

If you bought WorkwrK on AppSumo, paste your code under **AppSumo code** and choose **Redeem**. A code raises the workspace to the plan and seats it grants, for good, and never lowers either.
