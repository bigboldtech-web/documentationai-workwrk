## Ask a teammate

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the conversation" icon="message-square">
Open a private channel, a group or a direct message.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the teammate" icon="at-sign">
Type @ and pick the teammate under **AI teammates** in the list. Typing its name by hand does nothing.
</Step>
<Step title="Send" icon="send">
Write your request and send it.
</Step>
</Steps>

It reads up to the last 30 messages before yours, and answers where you asked, marked **via** its name. The answer is shown to the people who were in the conversation when it posted.

Anything else it wants to do that other people will see waits for your approval in your chat with it, and your Inbox gets a row. Each ask uses one of your AI questions.

## Why can't I ask a teammate here?

The @ list says why under **AI teammates**:

- **A public channel.** Teammates can't be asked in public channels, because everyone in the workspace can read the answer. Ask in a private channel, a group or a direct message.
- **A Guest is in the conversation.** Teammates can't be asked where a Guest reads.
- **You aren't a member.** Join the conversation first.
- **It is archived.**
- **More than 250 people are in it.**
- **You are a Guest.** Guests can't ask AI teammates.

Also:

- One teammate per message, and no files in a message that asks a teammate.
- You can ask teammates up to 5 times a minute.
- Talk must be on for the workspace. It is included from the Growth plan.
