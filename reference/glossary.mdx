Read this once. Several of these words mean something specific in WorkwrK and something different in other tools.

## People and structure

### Workspace
Your company's WorkwrK. Everyone in it holds one of four roles. One person can belong to more than one workspace; their account is anchored in one and reaches the others through a membership.

### Owner, Admin, Member, Guest
The four workspace roles. An **Owner** runs the company account: billing, ownership and security. An **Admin** runs the workspace day to day. A **Member** is an employee, who works in the Spaces they are added to and sees what is open to the whole company. A **Guest** is outside the company and sees only what is shared. See [Visibility and permissions](/reference/visibility-and-permissions).

### Tier
A Member's seniority, set on their row in Workspace settings, **Members**: Member, Team lead, Manager, Director, VP, Executive, People team or Agent. It decides which manager pages they open. It does not change what they can open in Spaces and Lists; sharing does that.

### Agent account
A Member tier for an account that works as a Member, can edit what it is given, never has Full access, and never exports or invites. It is not an AI teammate.

### People team
The tier for the people who look after everyone's people information. They can edit placement and reporting lines and read Members, Access and Scoring & reviews.

### Department
Where a job title and its people sit in the business. Managed under Teams, **Departments**, and in Workspace settings, **Structure**. A department is a container, not a permission.

### Job title
"Head of Customer Success", "Backend Engineer". A job title is where KRAs are defined. When somebody holds a job title, they inherit its KRAs and the published SOPs attached to those KRAs. Managed under Teams, **Job titles**.

### Reporting line and chain
Your manager, set on your record. Your chain is you plus everyone below you, all the way down. A manager's pages, such as My team, show their chain. A dotted line is a second manager relationship, used on the org chart and in KPI reviews.

### Tag
A label from one shared workspace list, managed in Workspace settings, **Task system**, **Tags**. The same tag can go on a task, an SOP or a person. A person tag is a segment: the Directory filters by it, and a goal, a pulse survey or an announcement can be aimed at it. Membership is worked out each time, never frozen. A tag is not a permission.

## Accountability

### KRA
Key Result Area. A permanent area of accountability attached to a **job title**, not to a person. "Customer retention". "Platform reliability". It has no number and no deadline, and it carries a **weight** from 0 to 100 saying what share of the job it is. Each holder inherits the weight and can have it adjusted. Weights are shown and totalled, and the total turns red past 100, but it is not enforced, and KRA weight does not yet weigh any score.

### KPI
Key Performance Indicator. A permanent gauge under a KRA, with a definition, a unit, a direction of good (**Higher is better**, **Lower is better** or **Hold the line**), a healthy line, how often it is recorded, and whether it is **Quantitative** (a number) or **Qualitative** (a 1 to 5 rating). A KPI is **owned** by the job title and graded, or **shared**, which it influences and is reviewed on. A north-star gauge is the one that matters most for its KRA.

### KPI reading
One measurement of one KPI by one person for one period. A reading moves through Not recorded, Submitted, Approved or Changes requested. A reading sent back stops counting at once.

### Compliance
On the Alignment board, the share of a person's current period readings that are Submitted or Approved. It is not a measure of whether they hit target. For SOPs, compliance is the share of assignments acknowledged.

## Goals

### Goal
A time-boxed objective with one owner, contributors and one progress number. Goals sit at Company, Department or Individual level and can nest.

### Owner
The single accountable person on a goal.

### Contributor
Anyone else attached to a goal: a person, a department, a job title or a tag. Department, job title and tag contributors are worked out each time the goal is read, so a new hire inherits their team's goals.

### Target
A measurable result under a goal, with a start value, a target value and a current value. Progress runs from the start toward the target, so a target that improves by going down rises as the number falls. A target can be measured by a KPI, which then supplies its number.

### Check-in
A dated update on a target: an increase or a decrease, and a note.

### Progress
One number per goal, worked out from its targets, or from its measured sub-goals. A goal with nothing measured shows a dash, not 0%. **Effort** and **On track?** are the automated signals beside it. See [Goals](/using/goals).

## Knowledge

### Doc
A page in the block editor, with sub-pages, version history, comments and sharing. Docs live under the Docs hub and open in place in Work.

### Canvas
A freeform board for diagrams, planning and system design, with **Design with AI**. Canvases were called whiteboards before.

### Notepad
Your private scratch pad, opened from the top bar. A Notepad note is yours alone: nobody else can open it, and it never shows in Docs or a doc picker.

### SOP
Standard Operating Procedure. A documented procedure, versioned and assignable. Four kinds: **Written**, **Step-by-step**, **Checklist** (which can be run) and **Click-capture** (recorded by the browser extension). SOPs are organised by categories, subcategories and tags. A published SOP under a KRA is assigned to everyone who holds that KRA's job title.

### SOP assignment
One person's obligation to read or run an SOP. They close it with **Mark as read**, which is what the SOP read rate counts.

### Run
One execution of a Checklist SOP, with a share link so someone outside the workspace can complete it.

### Policy
A document people acknowledge rather than execute, with an acknowledgement ledger.

### Contract
An agreement with parties, sent and signed by link.

### Notetaker
Paste a meeting transcript and get decisions, action items and attendees as a meeting note. It does not record audio.

## Work

### Space
The top container: a team, a client or a major area of work. A Space holds Folders and Lists. Who can open it is set in **Manage access**: **Invite only**, **Space members** or **Everyone at** your company.

### Folder
An optional grouping inside a Space. A Folder can be shared on its own, and **Restricted** stops it taking access from the Space.

### List
Where tasks live. Each List has its own statuses, fields and views. Board is its default view unless another is pinned.

### Task
One row on a List, with a status, assignees, dates, priority, tags, custom fields, subtasks, a checklist, comments and tracked time. A task opens as a drawer at its own address.

### Home List
The List a task was made in. A task can also be added to other Lists, where it carries a chip naming its home.

### Subtask
A task with another task as its parent. It goes wherever its parent goes.

### View
One way of drawing a List: List, Board, Calendar, Gantt, Timeline, Workload, Team, Cards, Hierarchy, Pivot, Chart, Dashboard, Map, Doc, Canvas, Form, File gallery or Activity. A view's settings are saved with it for everyone. **Pin as default view** makes it open first.

### Bird's eye
A Space, or a Folder, as columns of cards, one per List. **Focus** opens one List as columns of its statuses.

### My work and the Personal list
**My work** is every task assigned to you, grouped by when it is due. The **Personal list** under it is a private List only you can see.

### Everything
Every task you can open, across all Spaces.

### Template
A saved task, List or Space to start from. **Save as template** on each, and the Template Center to apply one.

### Trash
Where deleted and archived things go, on two tabs. Items stay until an Owner or Admin deletes them for good.

### Public link
A view-only address for one task, doc or SOP that works without signing in, while Workspace settings, **Access** allows public links. A table or form has its own public link in its share dialog.

## Sharing

### Manage access
The dialog in every `…` menu that says who can open a thing and at what level. Below Full access it reads **Who has access**.

### Full access, Can edit, Can edit assigned tasks, Can comment, Can view
The sharing levels. **Full access** changes settings, sharing, delete and transfer. **Can edit** adds and changes tasks, docs and rows. **Can edit assigned tasks**, on Lists only, reads and discusses every task and changes only tasks assigned to the person or that they made. **Can comment** reads and discusses, never changes. **Can view** is read only. A Space also has **Owner**: Full access plus every Restricted List in it.

### Restricted
On a Folder, List, doc or channel: only the people listed can open it, plus Admins, and it takes no access from above. A restricted channel has no admin read-around.

## AI

### Ask AI
The chat in the AI hub that answers and does work in the product, and asks first before anything other people would see.

### AI teammate
An AI helper with one job that you chat with. It works as you, sees only what you can, and asks before anything other people will see. See [AI teammates](/using/ai-teammates/overview).

### Workspace teammate
A teammate an Owner or Admin made for **Everyone in the workspace**. Everyone gets their own chat with it; nobody reads anyone else's.

### Approval card
The card a teammate or Ask AI shows before doing something other people will see. You **Approve**, **Edit**, **Deny** or **Approve and don't ask again**. A request nobody answers expires after 7 days.

### Don't ask
Your choice to let a teammate do one kind of thing without stopping for you. Inviting people always asks.

### Routine
A job a teammate does for you on a schedule, at most once an hour. Its reports arrive in your chat with it.

### Practice run
A run or a message that shows what a teammate would do without changing anything.

### Memory
Short facts a teammate keeps about how you work, visible on its Memory tab. On a workspace teammate, Owners and Admins can add shared memories for everyone.

### Group chat
One chat with two to five of your teammates.

### AI question
The unit your plan counts: each Ask AI message, each teammate message, routine run, group chat answer, answer in Talk, automation step and ask of another teammate. A total for the workspace, not a monthly allowance.

### Workspace agent
An agent an Owner or Admin added from the catalogue on the AI teammates page. It runs as whoever runs it, and any schedule it had is a routine now.

### Automation
A workflow in the AI hub: a trigger, conditions and actions, including **Ask an AI teammate**.

## Talk

### Channel
A conversation on a topic, findable by everyone at the company unless it is **Restricted: only people you add**.

### Direct message and group
A conversation with one person, or a few.

### Thread
Replies on one message, in a panel beside the conversation.

### Guest link
A link that lets someone outside the workspace join a conversation's calls, or a meeting, without an account.

## Tables and Forms

### Table
A spreadsheet in the Tables hub, with formulas, formatting, validation, pivots and typed columns, including links to other tables with lookups and rollups.

### Form
A set of questions whose answers land as tasks in a List or rows in a table.

## Time

### Timer
A running clock on a task. Stopping it writes an entry into your weekly timesheet.

### Timesheet
Your week of hours, with Draft, Submitted and Approved states, and **Log time** for an entry by hand. Managers see **Approvals** and **Team**.

### Workload
Open work per person, for capacity. A manager's **Workload** page shows their chain.

## Scoring

### Performance score
A monthly number per person, from KPI readings, the latest manager rating, peer feedback, self review, SOP compliance, goal progress and task delivery, plus a small kudos bonus. Parts with no data are skipped.

### Performance band
The name a score range gets: Exceptional, Strong, On track, Needs focus or At risk by default. Set in Workspace settings, **Scoring & reviews**.

### Review cycle
A performance round: self review, manager review, peer feedback and calibration, finalized into outcomes. See [Review cycles and cadences](/admin/reviews-and-cadences).

### Weekly review
A lighter, per-person weekly check-in with a manager approval state.

### Talent (9-box)
A performance by potential grid, filled by hand, from performance scores, or from a finalized review cycle.

## Settings and plans

### My settings and Workspace settings
The two doors of Settings. My settings is yours; Workspace settings is for Owners and Admins. See [Settings](/admin/settings).

### Seat
A person who can sign in, or an open invitation. Your plan sets how many seats a workspace has.

### Module
Talk and Tables, the premium parts of WorkwrK, turned on in Workspace settings, **Apps & modules** from the Growth plan.
