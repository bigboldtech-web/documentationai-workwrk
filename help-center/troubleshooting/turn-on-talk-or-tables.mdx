---
title: Why can't I turn on Talk or Tables?
description: Talk and Tables are premium modules, included from the Growth plan.
---

Talk (channels, direct messages, threads and calls) and Tables (spreadsheets) are premium modules. They are included from the Growth plan.

An Owner or Admin turns them on or off in Workspace settings, **Apps & modules**. On the free Starter plan, the switch says the module is included from the Growth plan. Change the plan in Workspace settings, **Plan & billing**, then turn it on.

While a module is off:

- it is hidden from the rail, and its pages can't be opened,
- AI teammates can't use its tools: a teammate can't read or post in Talk while Talk is off, or create or find tables while Tables is off.

A workspace that already had a module on keeps it.

Forms are not part of Tables. They work on every plan, from **All forms** in the Tables hub. See [Tables and Forms](/using/tables-and-forms).
