title: Review cycles and cadences

A **review cycle** is a performance round: a name, a type, a period, and the people it covers. It is not the same thing as a weekly review, which is a per person weekly check-in under **Teams**, **Weekly reviews**, and a separate flow.

Review cycles live under **Teams**, **Review cycles**.

## Who can open this

- **The People team**, **Owners** and **Admins** run every cycle in the workspace.
- **A manager** can start and run a cycle for their own reporting chain. A manager's cycle only ever reaches the people in that chain.
- **Everyone else** reaches their own review through the link they are sent when a cycle launches.

## Create a cycle

1. Go to **Review cycles**.
2. Choose **New cycle**.
3. Pick a type: **Monthly pulse**, **Quarterly**, **Annual appraisal**, **Probation** or **Performance plan**. The dialog fills in a name and a period from it, and both stay editable.
4. Choose **Who the cycle covers**. The People team, Owners and Admins pick **Everyone**, **Departments** or **Specific people**. A manager picks **Everyone who reports to me** or **Specific people who report to me**.
5. Choose **Create cycle**.

The cycle is created as a **draft**: **It starts as a draft. Nobody is asked for anything until you launch it.** A draft can be deleted with **Delete draft**, and nothing else is lost.

## Launch: this is the step that matters

A draft cycle has no reviews in it. Creating a cycle does not create anyone's review. **Launch cycle** does.

Before it launches, WorkwrK counts who the cycle covers and asks you to confirm that number. Launching then:

1. Creates a review for every active person the cycle covers, with their manager as the reviewer.
2. Moves the cycle from draft to active.
3. Tells each of them, in their Inbox and by email, with a link to their own review.

Things to know before you press it:

- A launch cannot be undone, because it has already asked people. If who the cycle covers changed between the count and your click, the launch is refused, so nobody is emailed a number you did not agree to.
- Removed people are never reviewed.
- **Cancel cycle** stops a launched cycle: **Nothing is deleted. The cycle stops and nobody is asked for anything more.**

## Running the cycle

A cycle has up to four tabs, each shown to the people it is for.

### My review

The person's own self review in this cycle, reached from the link they were sent. It shows their numbers for the period as proof points, then asks for a rating and a note per KRA and a reflection. It saves as you type.

### Team

The reviews you are the reviewer on, or, for the people running the cycle, everyone in it. **Open next review** jumps to the next one still waiting. For each person you record a rating and comments per KRA, the behavioural ratings, overall comments and a recommended outcome. A completed review can't be edited.

### Peer feedback

Ask peers to give feedback on a review, and answer the requests you were asked for.

### Calibration

Shown to the people running the cycle once it is in calibration. **Start calibration** moves the cycle there, and says first what is still outstanding.

The table lists each person with their **KPI**, **Self**, **Manager**, **Peers** and **Composite** scores, their **Band**, a **Calibrated** score you can set, their **Potential** and their **Outcome**. The calibrated score, when set, is what later reads as the person's manager rating.

## Finishing a cycle

**Finalize outcomes**, on the Calibration tab, is the only way to complete a cycle. It finalizes every review in your reach that carries an outcome, with its score: the calibrated score when set, otherwise the composite.

- **Also place these people on the talent grid** is on by default. It places each finalized person on **Talent (9-box)** for this cycle, from their score through the performance bands and their potential (Medium when unset). A placement someone made by hand for the same period is never overwritten.
- Only the people finalized are told, and only then.
- The cycle is marked complete only when every review in it is complete. If some still lack an outcome, or are outside your reach, the cycle stays in calibration and you are told how many remain.

Once a review is complete you can produce its appraisal letter: a printable letter with the company header, scores, band, a hike recommendation and the assessment details. Only the person reviewed, their reviewer, and the people who manage them can open it.

## Cadences and scoring

Workspace settings, **Scoring & reviews** holds four cards, each with **Reset to defaults**. One **Save** bar saves the cards you changed. Owners and Admins change them; managers and the People team can read them.

### Review cadence

One row per cadence: weekly check-in, monthly pulse, quarterly review and annual appraisal. Each says whether it is on, whether a draft cycle opens automatically when its day comes, which day that is, and how many days ahead reminders start. By default weekly, monthly and quarterly are on and annual is off.

What opening automatically does: when a cadence's day comes, a **draft** cycle for that period is created and the people who run cycles are told. It stops there. **Someone still has to launch it.** Weekly check-ins are per person and created as they are needed.

### Score weights

**How each part weighs into a person's review score.** Four parts: **KPI attainment**, **SOP compliance**, **Behavioral rating** and **Peer feedback**, 40, 20, 30 and 10 by default. They must add up to 100; the card shows the total and refuses to save otherwise.

The monthly performance score on profiles is a separate number: it also weighs the manager and self parts, which stay as they are, plus goal progress, task delivery and a small kudos bonus. It skips any part with no data, so a person with no peer feedback is not penalised for the missing part.

### Performance bands

**Score ranges that name a composite score.** Defaults are Exceptional 90 to 100, Strong 75 to 89, On track 60 to 74, Needs focus 40 to 59, and At risk 0 to 39. Add and remove rows as you need. Every band must sit inside 0 to 100, and ranges can't overlap. The bands name scores on reviews, in calibration and on the talent grid.

### Behavioural anchors

The five words of the rating scale the manager review shows, from 1 to 5. Defaults are "Needs significant improvement", "Below expectations", "Meets expectations", "Exceeds expectations" and "Role model". Exactly five are required.
