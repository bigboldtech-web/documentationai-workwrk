## Group chats

A group chat is one chat with two to five of your teammates. Each one answers as itself, reads what the others said, works as you, and asks you first, just as it does in its own chat.

### Start a group chat

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the New menu" icon="plus">
On the AI teammates page, choose **New**, then **New group chat**.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick the teammates" icon="users">
Pick two to five of your teammates. Two teammates in one group can't share a name.
</Step>
<Step title="Name it, if you like" icon="pencil">
For example, **Offsite crew**. Leave the name empty and the group is named after its first three teammates.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it" icon="check">
Choose **Create group chat**. It opens, and it shows in your list under Chats.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Who answers

- **Name a teammate with @** to ask it: type @ and its name, or pick it from the list above the composer, **Teammates in this group**. Up to three named teammates answer one message, the first three you name.
- **Name nobody**, and the group's lead answers: your Chief of Staff, when the group has one, otherwise the first teammate that can answer. The composer says which.
- A paused or removed teammate is skipped, with a line such as **Project Manager didn't answer: it is paused.**

Each teammate that answers uses one AI question. A message that names three teammates uses three.

### Approvals in a group chat

A teammate's requests wait on a card in the group chat. Your **Don't ask** choices for each teammate apply here as in its own chat. Group chats write no Inbox rows, because you are in the chat. Group chats have no Practice run. See [Approvals and Don't ask](/using/ai-teammates/approvals).

### Manage the group

The button with the number of teammates opens the group's menu:

- **Add teammate** adds one, up to five.
- **Remove** takes one out. Anything it is still waiting on for your approval in this group is cancelled, and its answers so far stay. A group keeps at least two teammates: to go below that, leave it instead.
- **Rename** changes the group's name.
- **Leave group chat** removes the group from your list. Anything still waiting for your approval in it is cancelled.

You can have up to 20 group chats.

## Ask your other teammates

A teammate with the tool **Ask your other teammates** can hand part of a request to another of your teammates, read its answer, and tell you what it said. A new Chief of Staff made from the template starts with this tool. For any other teammate, tick it on the **Tools and approvals** tab.

How it works:

- It asks only when you ask it something in your chat with it, or in a group chat. Routines, Talk and automations never ask other teammates.
- It can ask up to three teammates in one answer.
- The teammate it asks works as you and starts from the request alone: it does not read your chat or its memories, your Talk messages or your Inbox.
- Anything the asked teammate would do that other people see waits for your approval in **its own** chat with you, whatever you chose there. Your chat with the first teammate shows a line such as **Project Manager is waiting for your approval:** followed by what it wants to do, with an **Open** link to the card.
- The asked teammate's chat shows **Asked by** followed by the teammate that asked and the request, then its answer.
- Each ask uses one AI question, and counts toward the asked teammate's own monthly limit if it has one.

It can't ask a teammate that is paused, or itself. If two of your teammates share a name, it can't tell which you mean: rename one first. In a practice run, it only says **Would ask** followed by the teammate's name.
