This page covers the day-to-day mechanics of the work hierarchy: making the containers, putting tasks in them, and editing a task. For what the containers mean, read [Core concepts](/getting-started/core-concepts) first.

---

## The Work sidebar

Everything in the work hierarchy is reachable from **Work** on the rail. Its sidebar shows, top to bottom: **Home**, **My work** (with your **Personal list** under it), **Inbox**, **Activity**, **Everything**, your **Favorites**, your goals (**My goals**, **Team goals**, **Company goals**, **My KRAs & KPIs**), **Dashboards**, **Templates**, **Trash**, and then your **Spaces**.

Click the chevron on a Space row to expand it. The tree loads that Space's Folders, Lists, Docs, tables and canvases the first time you open it and remembers what you expanded. Following a link to a Folder or List opens its branch for you. **Browse all Spaces** lists every Space you can open.

### Creating containers

- **New Space**: the `+` next to the **Spaces** header, or the **New Space** row at the foot of the list. You see it when you may create Spaces: Owners, Admins, and Members whose tier is Team lead, Manager, Director, VP or Executive.
- **New Folder or List inside a Space**: open the Space's `…` menu, then **New**.
- **New List inside a Folder**: the Folder's `…` menu, then **New**, then **List**.

The New Space wizard has two steps. Step 1 is the basics: icon, colour, name, description and **Restrict this Space** (**Only you and the people you share it with can open it. Change this later in Share.**). Step 2 is the workflow: a preset, the Space owner, which views to create, the starting statuses, and which features are on.

### Features decide what a Space's tasks can do

A Space's features decide whether Priority, Tags, Time tracking and Custom fields appear at all. Turn one off in the Space's `…` menu, **Features**, and that field disappears from the List columns, the Board cards, the task drawer and the row menus for every List in that Space. It does not delete data, it hides the controls.

### Dragging things around

In the sidebar you can drag:

- a **List** into a Folder, or onto the Space row to move it back to the Space root
- a **Folder** into another Folder, or onto a Folder row's top or bottom edge to reorder it among its siblings
- a **Doc** into a Folder or to the Space root
- a **Space** row up or down to reorder your Spaces

Reordering saves at once. If the save fails, the tree reloads so what you see is always what is stored.

## The `…` menus

Every Space, Folder and List row has the same `…` menu, on right-click or from its hover button. A row you can't use is left out, never greyed.

| Row | Space | Folder | List | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Add to favorites, Pin to top | Yes | Yes | Yes | Everyone who can open it |
| New | Yes | Yes | | Those who can add things there |
| Rename, Color & icon | Yes | Yes | Yes | Full access |
| Copy link | Yes | Yes | Yes | Everyone who can open it |
| Manage access, or Who has access | Yes | Yes | Yes | Manage access at Full access, Who has access below it |
| Features | Yes | | | Full access |
| Statuses, Fields, Default task type, Default values, Conditional colors | | | Yes | Full access |
| About | | Yes | Yes | Everyone who can open it |
| Templates | Yes | Yes | Yes | Full access |
| Automations… | Yes | Yes | Yes | Can edit |
| Mute notifications | Yes | | Yes | Everyone who can open it |
| Hide from sidebar | Yes | | | Everyone who can open it |
| Move, Duplicate, Archive, Delete | Yes | Yes | Yes | Full access |

A Space or Folder you see only because something inside it was shared with you is a path, not a place you were given. Its menu has **Copy link** and nothing else.

**Duplicate** makes a copy and asks one question, **Include tasks**, which starts off: you get the shape of the thing without its tasks unless you tick it. **Move** moves it to another place you can add to.

## Sharing

Share a Space, Folder, List, doc, table, form or canvas from its `…` menu with **Manage access**. The person you add sees what you shared and the path to it, nothing more. Each thing offers its own levels:

| Thing | Levels you can give |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Owner, Full access, Can edit, Can view |
| Folder | Full access, Can edit, Can view |
| List | Full access, Can edit, Can edit assigned tasks, Can comment, Can view |
| Doc | Full access, Can edit, Can comment, Can view |
| Table | Full access, Can edit |
| Canvas, Form | Full access, Can edit, Can view |

Sharing flows down from a Space to its Folders and Lists, and a share only adds to what someone already has. **Restricted** on a Folder or List stops it taking access from above. The full rules are in [Visibility and permissions](/reference/visibility-and-permissions).

## Creating tasks

Several routes, all landing in the same place:

- **Add task** on the List toolbar opens the full create-task dialog with that List chosen.
- **+ Add Task** at the foot of the List view types a task inline.
- The add button on a Board column creates a task already in that status.
- **+** on a Calendar day creates a task due that day.
- **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + K** opens the create-task dialog from anywhere. The **Create** button in the top bar has **Task** too.

## The task drawer

Click a task and it opens as a drawer over the List, at its own address, `/item/` followed by the task's id. Copy that address and anyone who can open the task lands on it; loading it directly opens the task as a full page. Both show the same task, so nothing is missing in either.

Every field saves as you change it. There is no Save button.

### Fields

The field strip starts with four fields: **Status**, **Assignees**, **Due date** and **Priority**. **+ Add field** adds the rest: **Start date**, **Estimate**, **Time tracked**, **Tags**, **KRA / KPI** and **Watchers**, plus the List's own custom fields.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | The List's own status set. |
| **Assignees** | One person or several. The first is the task's owner. |
| **Due date** and **Start date** | Open the date planner (see below). |
| **Priority** | Urgent, High, Normal, Low, or cleared. Hidden if the Space's Priority feature is off. |
| **Estimate** | Free text like `2h 30m`, `90m` or `1.5h`. |
| **Time tracked** | Start and stop a timer, with your running total. Hidden if Time tracking is off. |
| **Tags** | Tags shared across the workspace. Hidden if Tags is off. |
| **KRA / KPI** | Ties this task to a KRA or KPI. Picking a KPI carries its KRA with it. |
| **Watchers** | People told about changes without being assigned. |

The description below the fields takes bold, italic, bulleted and numbered lists, links and @mentions.

### Several assignees

A task can carry several people, up to 50. In **Assignees**, choose **Add people…** and pick as many as you need. The first person is the task's owner. Assigning someone a task lets them open it and change it, even if they can't open the rest of its List.

In a List or Board, the people offered are those who can already reach the List. **Assign to me** in a task's `…` menu adds you.

### Dates, reminders and repeat

The **Due date** field opens a three-tab planner:

- **Date** sets and clears the start and due date and time, with quick chips and a month grid.
- **Reminder** sets one or more reminders on the task, relative to the due date or at a custom time. They show in the top bar's reminders bell.
- **Repeat** sets recurrence: how often, what triggers the next one, when it ends, and the status to set when it repeats. Edits apply when you choose Save.

The two repeat triggers behave differently:

- **After completion** rolls *this same task* forward the moment you mark it done.
- **On schedule** leaves this task alone and creates a fresh copy each cycle. The copies are made by an hourly job, so the next one appears on its schedule rather than instantly.

### Below the description

Empty sections stay out of the way behind **Add to task** rows until you use them.

- **Subtasks** are real tasks with this one as their parent. Add them inline, and drag them up and down to reorder.
- **Checklist** is a list of text items with tick boxes, kept on the task. Drag items to reorder.
- **Related** links notes, canvases, files, SOPs and other tasks.
- **Comments** and **Activity** come last. Type `@` in a comment to mention someone. Activity is the history: created, status changed, renamed, assignees changed, fields updated, archived.

Who hears what: a task's new owner is told it is theirs; a status change tells the owner, plus everyone who watches the task or commented on it; a comment tells the assignees and the watchers; and a mention tells the person mentioned. Nobody is notified about their own action, and each person's notification settings are respected.

## Tasks in more than one List

A task has one home List, and can also show in other Lists, so one piece of work can sit in a project and in a team's queue at once.

- **Add it**: in the task's `…` menu, choose **Add to another List…**, or use the Lists chip in the task's header.
- **See where it is**: the header chip reads **In** followed by how many Lists. It names the home List with a **Home** tag, and marks the List you have it open in with **Here**.
- **In the other Lists**, the task carries a small chip naming its home List (or **Also in another List** when you can't open the home).
- **Take it out**: **Remove from this List** in the task's `…` menu in that List, or **Remove** on its row in the chip. That needs edit access to that List or to the home List.

Changes you make in any List change the one task. From a List the task only shows in, **Archive** is not offered, and **Delete** reads **Delete everywhere**. Tasks on a Personal list can't be added to other Lists, and a subtask goes wherever its parent goes.

## A public link for one task

Share one task with someone outside the workspace, read only, without them signing in.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Public link" icon="link">
In the task's `…` menu, choose **Public link**.
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it on" icon="toggle-right">
Turn on **Anyone with the link can view**, then **Copy link**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose how long it lasts" icon="calendar-clock">
**No end date**, 7, 30 or 90 days. **Show assignees and comments** is off unless you turn it on.
</Step>
</Steps>

Anyone with the link sees the task's title, status, priority, dates, description, checklist and subtasks, and your workspace's name and logo. They can't change anything, and they never see files or emails. Turn the switch off and the link stops working.

You can share a task this way when you can add tasks to its List, and while your workspace allows public links: Workspace settings, **Access**, **Public links**. Tasks on a Personal list, tasks in Trash and meeting tasks can't be shared publicly, and the dialog says why.

## The task `…` menu

The same menu opens everywhere: List rows, Board cards, the drawer, and the chips, bars, cards and tree rows of the other views. Rows you can't use are left out.

**Open**, **Open in new tab**, **Ask AI about this task**, **Mark complete** or **Reopen**, **Assign to me**, **Rename**, **Copy link**, **Copy task ID**, **Duplicate**, **Move to list…**, **Add to another List…**, **Task type**, **Set reminder**, **Start timer**, **Watch**, **Save as template**, **Share**, **Public link**, then **Remove from this List**, **Archive** and **Delete**.

### Archive and Delete

Both go to the one Trash in the Work sidebar, on different tabs.

- **Delete** moves the task and its subtasks to Trash, on the **Deleted** tab. Restore it from there. Items stay in Trash until an Owner or Admin deletes them for good.
- **Archive** hides the task from its List and keeps it on Trash's **Archived** tab, where you can restore it. Archived items never expire.

Trash shows each person what they can restore. **Delete permanently** and **Empty trash** are for Owners and Admins.

## Working on many tasks at once

Select rows with the tick box in the List view, or cards on the Board, and an action bar appears at the foot of the screen:

- **Set status** to any of the List's statuses
- **Priority**, or clear it
- **Set due date**, or clear it
- **Set owner** to someone who can reach the List, or **Clear assignees**
- **Archive** the selection
- **Delete** the selection (to Trash)
- **Clear selection**

If part of a bulk action fails, the bar says which tasks did not change.

## Moving tasks around

- On the **Board**, drag a card to another column to change its status, or up and down to reorder it.
- In the **List** view, drag rows up and down.
- Changes other people make, such as a task they move, appear on your screen without a refresh.

---

## Places that show tasks across everything

- **Everything** shows every task you can open across all Spaces, grouped by status. Clicking a title opens the task, and the chip after it goes to its List.
- **My work** is every task assigned to you, grouped by when it is due (Overdue, Today, This week, Next week, No date), with List, Board, Calendar, Gantt, Timeline and Sprint views.
- **Personal list**, under My work, is a private List that only you can see and that works like any other List.
