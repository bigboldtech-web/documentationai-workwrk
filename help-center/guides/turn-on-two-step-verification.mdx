---
title: Turn on two step verification
description: Add a code from an authenticator app to your log in, and keep backup codes in case you lose your phone.
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Security" icon="shield-check">
Open My settings, then **Security**.
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it on" icon="toggle-right">
Next to **Two step verification**, choose **Turn on**.
</Step>
<Step title="Scan the code" icon="qr-code">
Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy or any authenticator app. If you can't scan it, choose **Can't scan? Enter this key**.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter a code" icon="hash">
Type the 6 digit code the app shows. Codes change every 30 seconds.
</Step>
<Step title="Save your backup codes" icon="key">
Save your 8 backup codes somewhere safe. They are shown once.
</Step>
</Steps>

From then on, WorkwrK asks for a code from the app each time you log in with your password.

## Lost your phone?

Log in with one of your backup codes instead of the app's code. Each backup code works once. Then, on **Security**, choose **Get new backup codes**, or turn two step verification off and on again with your new phone.

If you have no backup codes left, ask your workspace's Owner or Admin for help.

## Can't turn it off?

If your workspace requires two step verification for your role, **Turn off** is not offered.

## Logging in with Google

Once two step verification is on for you, or your workspace requires it for your role, logging in with Google is refused with **Signing in to this account needs two step verification.** Log in with your email and password, then your code. If you have never set a password, choose **Forgot your password?** on the log in page to set one.

More in [Sign-in and security](/using/account-security).
