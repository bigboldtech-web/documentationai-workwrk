---
title: Why can't I see this?
description: The workspace roles, how sharing decides who opens a Space, Folder or List, who sees whose people data, and why an app may be missing from your rail.
---

If something is missing in WorkwrK, it is almost always one of three rules: it was not shared with you, you are not in the right reporting line for someone's people data, or the app is hidden from your rail. Work through them in that order.

## Your role

Everyone in a workspace is an **Owner**, an **Admin**, a **Member** or a **Guest**.

- **Owners** and **Admins** open every Space and every Workspace settings page.
- **Members** work in the Spaces they are added to and see what is open to the whole company.
- **Guests** see only what is shared with them.

A Member also has a tier (Member, Team lead, Manager, Director, VP, Executive, People team or Agent), which decides which manager pages they open. It does not change what they can open in Spaces and Lists.

## Spaces, Folders and Lists

Whether you can open a piece of work is decided by **sharing**, from its `…` menu, **Manage access**.

| Thing | You can open it when |
| --- | --- |
| A Space | You are listed in it, or it is open to **Everyone at** your company, or you are an Admin |
| A Folder | You can open its Space, or it was shared with you on its own; a **Restricted** Folder opens only to the people listed, plus Admins |
| A List | You can open its Folder or Space, or it was shared with you; a **Restricted** List opens only to the people listed, plus Admins; a List open to **Everyone at** your company opens to all |
| A task | You can open its List, or it is assigned to you, or you made it |

Sharing flows down from a Space to its Folders and Lists, and never climbs: being given a List does not give you the Space above it. You see the List, and the path to it.

To be added, ask someone with Full access on the Space, Folder or List to open its `…` menu, choose **Manage access**, and add you. The level they give you decides what you can do: **Full access**, **Can edit**, **Can edit assigned tasks** (Lists only), **Can comment** or **Can view**.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Seeing a List is not the same as editing it. If you can read a List but every change is refused, you hold **Can view** or **Can comment** on it. Ask for **Can edit**. A task assigned to you is yours to change whatever your level on the List, unless Can comment is all you hold there.
</Callout>

## People data

A person's KRAs and KPIs, goals, reviews, assets and scores show only to the person, their reporting chain, the People team, Directors and above, and Owners and Admins. Everyone else sees the directory card: name, job title, department and manager.

Two consequences people trip over:

- A manager with **no reports** sees only themselves on My team and Workload. Reporting lines are set per person. An Owner, Admin or the People team fixes it on the person's record, or in **Teams**, **Org chart** with **Edit reporting lines**.
- The chain is the *reporting* line, not the department. Sharing a department with someone does not put them in your chain.

## Goals

- Company goals are visible to everyone.
- Your own goals, and goals whose contributors include you (directly, or through your department, job title or a tag), are visible to you.
- A manager sees the goals of anyone in their chain.
- Directors and above, the People team and Admins see every goal.

Opening a goal you are not entitled to see shows a not found page, so a missing goal and a forbidden goal look the same from outside. That is deliberate.

## Why an app is missing from the rail

Owners and Admins decide what the rail shows in Workspace settings, **Apps & modules**: an app can be hidden, or shown only to **Managers and up**, **People team and Admins** or **Admins only**. Talk and Tables also show only while the module is on.

So an app you expected may be hidden for your tier, or turned off for the workspace. Ask an Owner or Admin.

## Quick diagnosis

| Symptom | Most likely cause |
| --- | --- |
| A Space others talk about is not in my sidebar | It is invite only or for Space members, and you are not listed |
| A List inside a Space I can see is missing | The List, or its Folder, is **Restricted** |
| I can open a List but can't change anything in it | You hold Can view or Can comment on it |
| A colleague's record shows only a card | They are not in your chain, and you are not People team, Director or above, or an Admin |
| A whole app is missing from the rail | It is hidden or limited in Apps & modules, or its module is off |
| A manager page sends me to my own record | Your tier is Member |
