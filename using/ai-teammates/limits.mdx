## What a teammate can do

A teammate can use only the tools you tick for it, and only as far as your own access goes. The tools sit in four groups, as the **Tools** list shows them.

**Look things up** (never asks)

| Tool | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Find tasks, Find people, Find meetings, Find goals, Find SOPs, Find contracts, Find forms, Find tables | Only what you can open. |
| Read your KRAs, Read your KPIs, Read your assigned SOPs, Read your weekly reviews, Read your team's progress | Your own records, and your team's where you manage one. |
| Read your Inbox | Your own notifications. |
| Read your Talk messages | Only conversations you are in. It never marks anything read. |
| Ask your other teammates | Asks one of your other teammates and reads its answer. See [Group chats and asking other teammates](/using/ai-teammates/group-chats). |

**Make and change your own work** (runs at once unless you choose Ask me first)

| Tool | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Create tasks | Asks first when it is for someone else. |
| Change tasks | Status, due date, priority and owner. Asks first unless the task is on your own Personal list and nobody else is on it. |
| Comment on tasks | Asks first unless the task is on your own Personal list and nobody else is on it. Nobody is pinged by name. |
| Add to docs | Adds a section at the end. Earlier versions are kept. Asks first on any doc but your own notepad. |
| Draft SOPs | Makes a draft. |
| Schedule meetings | Asks first when anyone else is invited. |
| Create goals | Asks first for a goal owned by someone else, or a Department or Company goal. |
| Remember things, Forget things | See [Memory](/using/ai-teammates/memory). |
| Set up routines | Runs on a schedule you choose. Each run uses one AI question. See [Routines](/using/ai-teammates/routines). |

**Things other people will see** (asks first unless you choose Don't ask)

| Tool | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Create docs, Create forms, Create tables | Everyone in the workspace can open and edit a new one. |
| Move tasks between Lists | |
| Post in Talk | Don't ask is offered only for one conversation at a time. |
| Send kudos | Everyone in the workspace can see kudos. |
| Create KRAs, Create KPIs | |

**Always asks first**

| Tool | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Invite people | The invitation is sent when you approve. |

Tools that need a module show only while it is on: Talk for **Post in Talk** and **Read your Talk messages**, and Tables for **Create tables** and **Find tables**. Some Ask AI tools, such as tracking contracts and planning sprints, can't be given to a teammate yet.

## What a teammate can't do

- See or change anything you can't. Every tool runs with your access, as you.
- Act for anyone but the person using it. On a workspace teammate, each person's chat, memories, routines and approval choices are their own.
- Do anything other people would see without your approval, unless you chose **Don't ask** for it. Invitations always ask.
- Delete things, or change who has access to anything. It has no tools for either.
- Open or attach files, or browse the web. It has no tools for these.
- Ping people. Its comments and Talk posts mention nobody, and links in them become plain words.
- Run a routine more than once an hour, or set up a routine from inside a routine.

Everything a teammate does is recorded in the workspace's audit log as the teammate working for you, for example **Chief of Staff (for Priya Shah): Created task "Call Acme"**.

## What uses AI questions

Your workspace's plan includes a number of AI questions. A teammate uses one for each of these:

- a message you send it, and each time it carries on after you approve something in the chat,
- each teammate that answers in a group chat,
- each routine run, including **Run now** and **Practice run**,
- a message sent with **Practice run** on,
- each time it asks another of your teammates,
- each answer in Talk,
- each run of an automation step that asks it.

One message is one question, however many steps the teammate takes to answer. Denying a request, deciding from the Inbox, and changing its settings or memories use none. A request that fails before the AI answers is not counted.

Every Ask AI message uses a question too. Workspace settings, **Plan & billing**, shows how many your workspace has used, under **AI questions used, in total**.

### A teammate's own monthly limit

Whoever manages a teammate can set a **Monthly limit** on its **Instructions** tab: the most AI questions it may use in a calendar month, on top of the plan. When it reaches the limit, it says it has used its questions for the month and can answer again on the 1st of next month (UTC), or whoever manages it can raise the limit.

## Limits on each plan

| | Starter | Growth | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Teammates of your own, per person | 3 | 20 | 50 |
| Workspace teammates, shared by everyone | 3 | 30 | 100 |
| AI questions for the workspace, in total | 50 | 500 | 2,000 |
| Talk and Tables | Not included | Included | Included |

AI questions are a total for the workspace, not a monthly allowance. On the free Starter plan, each person also has 50 free questions in total, counted across every free workspace they use. A workspace on the Enterprise plan has no limit on teammates or AI questions.

When a limit is reached:

- **Teammates**: making another one says how many you have, the most the plan allows, and that an Owner or Admin can change the plan in Workspace settings, **Plan & billing**.
- **AI questions**: teammates and Ask AI stop answering until the plan changes, and routines pause with a reason. Resume them after the plan changes.

See [Plans and billing](/admin/plans-and-billing) for the rest of what each plan includes.

### Other limits

| What | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Routines | 10 with one teammate, 30 across all your teammates |
| Group chats | 20 per person, each with 2 to 5 teammates |
| Memories | 100 of yours per teammate, 100 shared on a workspace teammate |
| Requests waiting for you | 100 at once, and 50 from one answer |
| Actions in one answer | 30 |
| Teammates asked in one answer | 3 |
| A request's wait for your decision | 7 days, then it expires |
| Asking teammates in Talk | 5 times a minute |
| Automation steps that ask a teammate | 20 a day for one automation (UTC) |
