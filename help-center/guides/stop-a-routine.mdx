---
title: Stop or pause a routine
description: Pause a teammate's routine for a while, or delete it for good.
---

A routine is a job an AI teammate does for you on a schedule. You can pause it and resume it later, or delete it.

## Pause a routine

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the teammate's settings" icon="settings">
In your chat with the teammate, choose **Settings**, then **Routines**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the routine's menu" icon="ellipsis">
Find the routine and open its menu.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Pause" icon="pause">
It stops running. Its row says it is paused. Choose **Resume** in the same menu to start it again.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also choose **Pause** on the routine's **Created routine** line in the chat.

## Delete a routine

In the same menu, choose **Delete**. **Delete this routine?** asks first. It stops running for good, and its past reports stay in the chat.

## Stop all of a teammate's routines at once

Whoever manages the teammate can pause the teammate itself, with the **On** switch on its **Instructions** tab or **Pause** in the chat header's **...** menu. While it is paused, all its routines skip their runs. They run again once it is turned on.

## Good to know

- Your routines are yours. On a workspace teammate, you see and stop only your own, and nobody else can stop yours.
- Each run uses one AI question, so pausing a routine you don't need saves questions.
- A routine can also pause by itself, for example when the workspace runs out of AI questions. See [Why did my routine pause?](/help-center/troubleshooting/why-did-my-routine-pause)
