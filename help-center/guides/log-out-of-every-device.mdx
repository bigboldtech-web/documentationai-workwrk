---
title: Log out of every device
description: End every session at once if you lost a device or think someone else has your password.
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Security" icon="shield-check">
Open My settings, then **Security**.
</Step>
<Step title="Log out everywhere" icon="log-out">
Under **Where you are logged in**, choose **Log out everywhere**, and confirm.
</Step>
</Steps>

It ends every session, on every device, including the one you are using. Each device has to log in again. A device that was open shows **You were logged out on every device. Log in again to keep working.**

If you think someone else knows your password, also choose **Change password** on the same page, and check **Recent security activity** for anything you didn't do.

More in [Sign-in and security](/using/account-security).
