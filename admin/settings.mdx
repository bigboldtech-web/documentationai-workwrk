Settings takes over the screen. The rail and the top bar stay; the sidebar and the page change to Settings. Choose **Back to app**, or press **Esc**, to return to where you were. **Find a setting** at the top of the Settings sidebar searches every page (Cmd/Ctrl + /).

## The two doors

Settings has two doors, and the split is built.

- **My settings** changes things only for you. Everyone has it. Open it from your avatar menu.
- **Workspace settings** changes the workspace for everybody. Owners and Admins open it from the avatar menu, and see a **Workspace settings** row at the foot of My settings, so the two doors are one click apart.

Members with a manager tier, and the People team, can also open three Workspace settings pages to read them: **Members**, **Access** and **Scoring & reviews**. In Members, a manager sees their own team. Nobody below Admin sees a control they can't save.

Each door has an **All settings** page that lists every setting it holds.

Old Settings addresses still work: each one redirects to the page that holds it now.

| Page | What it holds |

## Workspace settings

Workspace settings opens on **Overview**, then groups its pages under **Workspace**, **People**, **Work**, **Security & data** and **Billing**.

### Workspace

#### Overview

The landing page: a summary of the workspace, with links to the pages that change each part.

#### Identity & culture

**Your workspace name, your mission, and the defaults everyone starts with.** Four tabs:

- **Profile**: the **Workspace** name and domain, **About the business**, and **Branding** (the logo).
- **Culture**: **Mission and vision**, and **Values and the welcome splash**. See [Mission and values](/using/mission-and-values).
- **Appearance defaults**: the look every member starts with, and the controls you can lock so members can't change them.
- **Danger zone**: **Transfer ownership**, which only an Owner can use (**Make someone else an Owner of this workspace.**), and **Delete workspace**: **Everyone is signed out. WorkwrK support can restore it for 30 days; after that it is gone for good.**

#### Locale & work week

**Defaults for dates, money and the working week.** Region, dates and the week, and the working year. Each person can override the language, the formats and the week start in their own Preferences.

#### Apps & modules

**Turn capabilities on, and decide what shows in everyone's rail.**

- **Modules**: Talk and Tables, the premium modules, included from the Growth plan. See [Plans and billing](/admin/plans-and-billing).
- **The rail**: the order of the apps, which to hide, and who sees each (**Everyone**, **Managers and up**, **People team and Admins** or **Admins only**). Hiding an app takes it off people's rails. Nothing is deleted.
- **Pause all automations** pauses every automation in the workspace until you turn it off again. The row shows how many automation runs the workspace used this month.

### People

#### Members

Everyone in the workspace, with four tabs: **People**, **Guests**, **Teams** and **Pending invites**. Counts across the top show the Owners, Admins, Members, Guests and People team.

- Search, filter (role, department, office, job title, status, no manager, People team, inactive 30 or more days) and sort the list.
- Open a person's row to change their role, tier, placement or status, or to remove them. Select several to **Change role** or **Change department** at once.
- **Invite** sends invitations. **Invite rules** on Pending invites sets the **Allowed email domains**, the **Default role for invites** and the **Invitation expiry** in days.

See [People and invites](/admin/people-and-invites).

#### Structure

Five tabs: **Departments**, **Job titles**, **Offices**, **Profile fields** and **Org chart**.

#### Access

Two tabs:

- **Settings**: **How access works**, the model in plain words with live counts; the access settings; **Public links**, which decides whether SOPs, tasks and docs can have a public, view-only link; and access requests.
- **Who can do what**: every role and tier against what it can do, read only.

See [Visibility and permissions](/reference/visibility-and-permissions).

### Work

#### Task system

Three tabs: **Task types**, **Tags** and **Templates**. On **Tags** you can archive a tag or delete it; deleting a tag that is in use says how many records carry it, and offers to archive it instead.

#### Scoring & reviews

**How reviews run and how scores are worked out.** Four cards, each with **Reset to defaults**: **Review cadence**, **Score weights**, **Performance bands** and **Behavioural anchors**. See [Review cycles and cadences](/admin/reviews-and-cadences).

### Security & data

#### Security

**How people sign in to your workspace. Changes apply at their next check, within five minutes.** Two tabs:

- **Sign-in policy**: **Passwords** (minimum length, uppercase, number, symbol, and when passwords expire); **Sessions** (how long someone can be idle, and the longest a session lasts); **Two step verification** (**Require it for** a group of people); **Failed sign-ins** (the built-in rule is 8 failures, then 15 minutes; you can lock sooner or for longer); **Invitation domains**; and the **Danger zone** with **Log everyone out**.
- **Provisioning**: the SCIM endpoint and provisioning tokens, for adding and removing people from your identity provider. **Generate a provisioning token** shows the token once.

#### Data

Four tabs:

- **Export**: **Full workspace (ZIP)** holds people, Spaces, Folders and Lists; every task with its description, checklist, tags, custom fields and comments; Docs and SOPs as Markdown; Tables as CSV; goals and review cycles. It leaves out files and attachments, images inside Docs, canvases, forms, chat, Doc comments and review answers, and personal notes stay their owner's. There are also CSVs of **People**, **Timesheets** and the **Audit log**.
- **Import**: **People from a CSV**, where each row is checked first and everyone ready gets an invitation, and **A CSV into a table**.
- **Retention & privacy**: how long Trash and the audit log keep things, and **Privacy**: **AI features for everyone** (off hides every AI entry point in the workspace) and **AI fields in Lists**. Today nothing is removed from Trash or the audit log automatically; the page says so.
- **Trash**: the workspace Trash.

#### Audit log

Who did what, filterable, with tabs **All**, **Access**, **Security**, **Data** and **Settings**, and an export to CSV that keeps your filters. Work an AI teammate did is recorded as the teammate working for the person.

#### API & webhooks

**API keys** for anything that connects to the workspace from outside: generate a key (the secret is shown once), see when each was last used, and revoke it. See [Authentication](/api-reference/authentication).

### Billing

#### Plan & billing

Your plan, seats and AI questions used, and the way to change the plan. See [Plans and billing](/admin/plans-and-billing).
