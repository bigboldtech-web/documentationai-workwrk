## Invite someone

The **Invite people** dialog opens from three places:

- **Invite** in the Teams hub's Directory.
- Workspace settings, **Members**, the **Invite** button in the header.
- The workspace menu at the top of the rail.

The dialog says: **Teammates get an email with a link to join your workspace. Every invite can carry a role: its KRAs, KPIs and SOPs attach automatically.** It collects:

- **Email addresses**. It is a chip input, so you can paste or type several separated by commas, spaces or Enter. Anything that is not a valid email is flagged instead of silently dropped.
- **Access level**. Only the levels you may give are offered, for example **Employee: Standard employees** or **Admin: Full access to the workspace**. The Owner role is never offered here. See [Visibility and permissions](/reference/visibility-and-permissions).
- **This is an agent account**, for an account that works as a Member, can edit what it is given, never has Full access, and never exports or invites.
- **Department**, **Role** and **Reporting manager**, all optional. These place the hire so they do not arrive unassigned.
- **Personal message**, optional, quoted inside the invitation email.

**The role is the definition.** Pick a **Role** (a job title) and its KRAs, KPIs and published SOPs attach automatically when the person joins: **No per-item selection needed.** There are no KRA or SOP pickers in the dialog, and an invite does not need a KRA or an SOP to exist first.

One invitation is sent per email address. Successes are counted in the confirmation toast. Addresses that fail, such as someone already invited or already a member, stay in the chip row so you can fix and retry.

### What every invitation checks

- **The email domain.** Invitations go only to the workspace's allowed domains. The workspace's own domain is always allowed; Owners and Admins add others under **Invite rules**.
- **A free seat.** Every open invitation takes a seat. When the workspace is full, a new invitation is refused. See [Plans and billing](/admin/plans-and-billing).
- **Not already here.** An address already in the workspace, or with a live invitation, is refused. Resend that invitation from **Pending invites** instead. An expired one is renewed when you invite the address again.

### Invite rules

Workspace settings, **Members**, **Pending invites** has the **Invite rules** card:

- **Allowed email domains**: domains besides your own that invitations may go to.
- **Default role for invites**: **Member** or **Admin**.
- **Invitation expiry**: how many days an invitation works, from 1 to 90. The default is 7.

### What happens when they accept

The invitation link opens the **Join** page for your workspace, which shows exactly what they are joining.

- Someone new chooses their name and a password, under your workspace's password rules.
- Someone who already has a WorkwrK account logs in, then joins.
- Someone already signed in with the invited address joins in one click.

On joining, they get the access level, department, role, manager and office the invitation carried. If the invitation named a role, its KRAs and published SOPs are assigned at the role's weights.

If the link is out of date, the page says so and offers **Ask for a new invitation**. If the workspace has no free seat right now, the page says so, and the invitation keeps working once there is room.

A new joiner closes out each assigned SOP from the SOP's own page, with **Mark as read** in the header. That is the acknowledgement the SOP read rate counts, and it works for people with no SOP management rights at all.

### Managing pending invitations

Workspace settings, **Members**, **Pending invites** lists every invitation not yet accepted, with its email, role, whether it is to the **Workspace** or **A Space**, when it expires, and its status, **Pending** or **Expired**. Each row can be resent or revoked. A resend gives the invitation a new link and a fresh expiry. Accepted invitations can't be revoked.

Every invitation is recorded in the audit log.

### Inviting someone into a single Space

A Space's **Manage access** dialog can also invite by email. That invitation carries the Space and drops the person straight into it when they accept. It joins them to the workspace as a Member, not as a Guest.

### Inviting many people at once

Workspace settings, **Data**, **Import**, **People from a CSV** checks each row first, and everyone ready gets an invitation. Nobody joins until they accept, and everyone joins as a Member.

## The directory

**Teams**, **Directory** is the who's who, open to everyone in the company.

- Views: **All**, **New** (joined in the last 90 days), and for Owners, Admins and the People team, **No manager** and **Removed**.
- **Filter** by search, department, job title, office, who they report to, tags, seniority and deactivated people. **Sort** by name, joined, tenure or most reports, and switch between a table and cards.
- Click a person to open their record as a drawer over the list; open it in a new tab for the full page.

What a record shows depends on who is looking. Everyone sees the directory card. A person's KRAs and KPIs, goals, reviews and assets show to them, their reporting chain, the People team, Directors and above, and Owners and Admins. See [Your profile](/using/my-profile).

### Changing someone's details

- **Role and tier** are for Owners and Admins, in Workspace settings, **Members**, on the person's row. Only an Owner can make someone an Owner, and the workspace always keeps at least one Owner.
- **Job title**, **Department**, **Office**, **Reports to** and **Weekly capacity** can be changed by Owners, Admins and the People team.
- People editing their own record are told **Ask your manager or the People team to change your job title, department, office or manager.**

## Tagging people

A person's record has a **Tags** row. The people who manage the person can add and remove tags there; on your own record you can read your tags.

Tags come from the one workspace list in Workspace settings, **Task system**, **Tags**, where they can also be archived or deleted.

A person tag is not a permission. It is a segment, and four things read it:

- The Directory's tag filter.
- A **Goal**, which can name a tag as a contributor.
- A **pulse survey**, which can target a tag as its audience.
- An **Announcement**, which can target a tag as its audience.

In all four the membership is worked out each time it is read, not frozen when you created the goal or the survey. Tag someone next week and they are inside those audiences straight away; untag them and they drop out. Archived tags never count.

This is the tool for groups that are not a department and not a job title: a joiner cohort, a programme, a task force, a shift.

## The org chart

**Teams**, **Org chart** draws who reports to whom for the whole company, for every Member.

- **Filter** by search, department, office or job title, or show only people **Not linked to a manager**, or **Only my chain**.
- **Edit reporting lines**, for Owners, Admins, the People team and Directors and above, adds **Reports to** and dotted line pickers to every row. Each saves when you pick.
- The **...** menu has **Expand all**, **Collapse all**, display options and **Export CSV**.

## Offboarding

Removing someone is done from the **...** menu on their record, **Remove from workspace**, or from their row in Workspace settings, **Members**.

The dialog does real work before it asks you to confirm:

1. **What they still hold.** It lists their open work and their direct reports.
2. **Who takes it over.** **Hand their open work and reports to** starts on their manager, or on you. **Done work keeps its owner. Goals, KRAs and assets stay on the record for the People team to reassign.**
3. **Confirm.** Type their first name back, and they are removed.

If the hand-over fails, nothing is removed.

### What removal does

Removal can be undone, and nothing is lost:

- They can't sign in, and an open session stops working.
- All their history stays: tasks, docs, reviews, KPI numbers and kudos.
- They leave headcount and the Directory's main views, and show under **Removed**.
- They stop counting in any department, role or tag audience.

Members also offers **Deactivate** and **Reactivate**, which stop and restore someone's sign-in without removing them.

### Restoring

Open the person from the Directory's **Removed** view and choose **Restore**: **They can sign in again, and their history picks up where it left off.**

### Who can remove whom

Owners and Admins can remove anyone but themselves. Managers can remove people in their own reporting line, other than Owners and Admins. Nobody can remove themselves.
