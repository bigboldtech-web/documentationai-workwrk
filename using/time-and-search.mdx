Two unglamorous surfaces you will use every day. Both have edges worth knowing before you rely on them.

---

## Tracking time on a task

Time is tracked **against a task**. There are two ways to start:

- the **Time tracked** field in the task drawer: a start button and your running total
- **Start timer** in a task's `…` menu

Only one timer runs at a time per person. While one is running, the task's own Time tracked field shows the clock.

Click the total in the task drawer to open the full tracker: your recent sessions on this task.

### Time entered by hand

Stopping a running timer writes an entry into your weekly timesheet. To add time you forgot to track, use **Log time** on the Timesheets page, or **Time entry** in the Planner sidebar's **+** menu, which opens the same thing on this week.

---

## How tracked time becomes a timesheet

When you stop a task timer, WorkwrK:

1. Closes the session and stores its duration.
2. Finds, or creates, your **Draft** timesheet for the week that contains the stop time. Timesheet weeks start on Monday, in UTC.
3. Appends a time entry to it, on that day, described with the task's title and linked back to the task.

If the week's timesheet has already been submitted or approved, **nothing is written**. A locked week is never silently modified. Track the time, then talk to whoever approves your sheets.

## The Timesheets page

**Planner**, **Timesheets** opens on your weeks, with three views:

| View | Who can use it | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **My timesheets** | Everyone | Your weeks. |
| **Approvals** | Managers | Submitted weeks waiting on you. |
| **Team** | Managers | Your chain's weeks. |

Click a week to expand it and see its entries, with the task each entry came from. **Log time** adds an entry by hand.

Row actions depend on status:

- A **Draft** week can be **Submit**ted.
- A **Submitted** week can be **Retract**ed back to draft.
- In **Approvals**, a submitted week can be **Approve**d or **Reject**ed.

## Finding things

### Search (Cmd/Ctrl + K)

The search palette opens with **Cmd + K** (**Ctrl + K** on Windows and Linux) or the search box in the top bar: **Search or type a command…**. Type at least two characters and results come back live, as one ranked list.

**What search covers:**

- **Tasks**, **Lists**, **Spaces** and **Folders**
- **Docs**, **canvases**, **tables** and **forms**
- **People**
- **SOPs**, **goals**, **meetings**, **departments**, **ideas**, **policies** and **announcements**

Results are access-checked the same way the app is. A Space, List or doc you cannot open never appears, and a Notepad note shows only to its owner.

### The palette before you type

With an empty query the palette shows real, working rows: your recent pages, quick actions such as creating a task, a doc or a reminder, and navigation rows to Inbox, My work, the Planner, Goals, Docs and more. Every row either navigates or runs an action.

### Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + K** | Open the search palette. |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + K** | Create a task. |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + E** | New note. |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + J** | Ask AI. |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + \\** | Collapse or expand the sidebar. |
| **?** | The keyboard shortcuts overlay. |
| **g** then **1** to **8** | Go to the first to eighth hub on the rail. |
| **g** then **i**, **h** or **w** | Go to Inbox, Home or My work. |
| **Escape** | Close what is open. |

Pages have their own: on My work, **1** to **6** switch views and **f** opens Filter; in the Inbox, the arrow keys move between notifications, **e** clears one, **u** marks it unread and **s** snoozes it. My settings, **Keyboard shortcuts** lists every one by where it works.

### Precise ways to work a set of tasks

Search finds a task by title. These remain the sharper tools when you want many tasks at once:

- **Everything**: every task you can open across all Spaces, grouped by status, with the full List toolbar.
- **A List's own search**: the magnifier in the List toolbar filters that List's tasks by title as you type.
- **My work**: everything assigned to you, grouped by when it is due.
- **Filters** on any view, which are far more precise than text search: field, operator, value, joined with AND or OR, and savable.

### Finding docs, files and mentions

- The **Docs** hub has its own search, filters and views.
- **Files** has a search box, filters by type and uploader, and views for favorites, recent files and files in Spaces.
- **Mentions** collects every place in a doc or an SOP where someone mentioned you, with the surrounding text. Clicking a row jumps to the exact block.

## Inbox, and what reaches it

**Inbox** in the Work sidebar sorts notifications into **Primary**, **Other**, **Mentions**, **Snoozed** and **Cleared**.

Tasks write real notifications:

- **A task is assigned to you**, or handed to you.
- **A task you are assigned to changes status**; so does a task you watch or commented on.
- **Someone comments** on a task assigned to you, or on a task you follow.
- **Someone mentions you** in a comment or a doc.
- **A task is due today**: once a day in the morning, for each task assigned to you due that day.
- **An AI teammate is waiting for your approval**, from a routine, an answer in Talk or an automation.

Each of these has its own switch in My settings, **Notifications**, and you are never notified about your own action. Overdue notifications are not sent yet, so there is no **Overdue** switch; with **Show upcoming features** on, it is listed as coming soon.

A **notifications bell** sits in the top right, next to the reminders bell. It carries an unread count, opens your recent notifications, and can raise a desktop alert while the tab is in the background once you allow it from the **Desktop** tab. The avatar menu has **Do not disturb** and **Mute notifications** for an hour, until tomorrow, or until you turn it back on.
