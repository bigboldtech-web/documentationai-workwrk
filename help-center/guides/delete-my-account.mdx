<Callout kind="danger">Deleting your account can't be undone. Nobody, including your workspace's Owners and Admins, can bring it back.</Callout>

## Before you start

- **Download your data** if you want a copy: My settings, **Profile**, **Your data**, **Download**. See [Download a copy of your data](/help-center/guides/download-my-data).
- **If you are the only Owner or Admin** of a workspace you belong to, you can't delete your account yet. The dialog says **You are the only admin of** the workspace. Make someone else an admin first in Workspace settings, **Members**, or delete the workspace in Workspace settings, **Identity & culture**, **Danger zone**.

## Delete your account

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your profile" icon="user">
Open My settings, then **Profile**.
</Step>
<Step title="Find the Danger zone" icon="triangle-alert">
Under **Danger zone**, choose **Delete my account**.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm" icon="keyboard">
Type DELETE in the box, then choose **Delete my account**.
</Step>
</Steps>

You are logged out at once, on every device.

## What is erased

Your account is deleted in every workspace you belong to, not only the one you were in. The dialog says so.

- Your name, email, photo, phone, date of birth and password. You show as **Deleted User** from then on.
- Your notifications.
- The words of your chats with Ask AI and your AI teammates, in every workspace, and what each request a teammate asked you to approve would have sent or changed.
- What your AI teammates remembered about you.
- Your routines: they never run again, and their names and instructions are erased.
- The text of your AI questions. The count stays, because it is part of your workspace's plan.
- The network details, such as the IP address and device, on your activity.

Most of this happens at once. The words of a long AI history are erased in the background, usually within minutes.

## What stays with your workspace

- The tasks, docs and messages you created, shown as from a deleted user.
- Records the workspace keeps about its work, such as reviews, KPI records, kudos and action items, with your name removed.
- The record of what you did there, shown as **Deleted User**, without your network details. The workspace's own retention setting decides how long its audit log is kept.
- A record that you asked for your account to be deleted, which the law requires.

## Related

- Leaving one workspace but keeping your account is up to that workspace's Owners and Admins: they can remove you from it in Workspace settings, **Members**.
- To ask for anything else to be deleted, email privacy@workwrk.com.
