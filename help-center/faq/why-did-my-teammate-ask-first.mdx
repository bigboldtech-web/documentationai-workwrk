---
title: Why did my AI teammate ask first?
description: The reasons a teammate stops for your approval, and what you can change.
---

A teammate works as you. It asks first whenever what it is about to do would be seen by other people, so nothing goes out in your name without you knowing.

## The usual reasons

- **Other people will see it.** Posting in Talk, sending kudos, moving a task between Lists, creating a KRA or KPI, and creating a doc, form or table (everyone in the workspace can open and edit a new one) all ask first by default.
- **It reaches someone else.** A task for someone else, a meeting with anyone else invited, a goal owned by someone else, or a Department or Company goal.
- **The task or doc isn't only yours.** Changing or commenting on a task asks first unless the task is on your own Personal list and nobody else is on it. Adding to a doc asks first unless it is your own notepad.
- **It is an invitation.** Inviting people always asks. This can't be changed.
- **You chose Ask me first** for that tool.
- **Whoever manages a workspace teammate chose Ask everyone first** for that tool. The Tools and approvals tab says so.
- **It was asked from Talk, an automation or another teammate.** In those places it asks first for everything other people would see, whatever you chose, because you may not be watching.

## What you can change

If you are happy for it to go ahead on its own, choose **Don't ask** for the tool on the **Tools and approvals** tab, or **Approve and don't ask again** on the card. See [How do I let a teammate act without asking?](/help-center/guides/let-a-teammate-act-without-asking)

Reading never asks. If a teammate asked before only looking something up, it was about to do something else too: read the card to see what.

More in [Approvals and Don't ask](/using/ai-teammates/approvals).
