---
title: Memory
description: What an AI teammate remembers about how you work, where you see it, and how to change or delete it.
---

A teammate can remember short facts about how you like to work: your report day, who you report to, how you like updates written. It reads them before it answers you, so you don't have to say them again.

## How a teammate remembers

Tell it in the chat, for example: **Remember that I send my status on Mondays.** The teammate needs the **Remember things** tool, which every template starts with.

Every time it saves or changes a memory, the chat shows a line such as **Memory updated: Send my status on Mondays**, with a **See memory** link. When it forgets one, the line reads **Forgot:** followed by the memory's name. So you always see what it keeps.

Teammates also remember on their own when the instructions ask them to. The Chief of Staff template, for example, remembers how you like to work.

## See and change what it remembers

In the chat header, choose **Settings**, then **Memory**. Each memory shows its name, what it remembers and who it is for.

- **Add memory**: give it a **Name** (for example, **Report day**) and **What to remember** (for example, **Send my status on Mondays**), then **Save**.
- **Edit** or **Delete** a memory from its menu. **Delete this memory?** asks first. The teammate stops using it from your next message.

To have the teammate forget something from the chat, ask it to, for example **Forget my report day.** It needs the **Forget things** tool.

## Only you, or everyone

- **Only you**: memories saved in your chat, or added by you on the Memory tab. They are yours alone. On a workspace teammate, nobody else, not even an Owner or Admin, sees your memories or has them used in their chats.
- **Everyone**: on a workspace teammate only. Its Owners and Admins can add shared memories that it uses with everyone. Everyone sees them on the Memory tab, and only the Owners and Admins can change them.

A teammate that is just yours keeps only your own memories.

## Where memories are used

A teammate uses your memories in your own chat with it, in group chats and in your routines.

It does not use your memories, or your chat with it, when it answers in Talk, runs in an automation, or is asked by another of your teammates. Each of those starts from the request alone.

What a teammate remembers is treated as notes, never as instructions to follow.

## Limits

- Up to 100 memories for you with one teammate, and up to 100 shared memories on a workspace teammate. Past that, the teammate says it already remembers 100 things and asks you to forget some first.
- A name can be up to 80 characters, and what it remembers up to 500.
- At most 40 memories, and 3,000 characters of them, go into one answer: your own first, then shared ones, each the most recently changed first.

When you delete your account, the memories teammates kept for you are deleted. See [How do I delete my account?](/help-center/guides/delete-my-account)
