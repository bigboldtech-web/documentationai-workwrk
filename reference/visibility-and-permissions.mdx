title: Visibility and permissions
description: The four workspace roles, member tiers, the sharing levels on Spaces, Folders, Lists and docs, and who sees whose people data.
---

WorkwrK answers two different questions, and it is worth keeping them apart:

- **Who can open a piece of work**: a Space, Folder, List, task, doc, table, form or canvas. That is decided by **sharing**.
- **Who can see a person's people data**: their KRAs and KPIs, goals, reviews and scores. That is decided by **the reporting line** and a few roles.

Both sit on top of each person's **workspace role**.

## Workspace roles

Everyone in a workspace holds one of four roles. Workspace settings, **Access**, explains them with a live count of each.

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Runs the company account: billing, ownership and security. |
| **Admin** | Runs the workspace day to day: opens every Space, invites people and uses every Workspace settings page. |
| **Member** | An employee: works in the Spaces they are added to and sees what is open to the whole company. |
| **Guest** | Outside the company, sees only what is shared. |

Owners and Admins open every Workspace settings page, including **Plan & billing**, **Security** and **API & webhooks**. Members with a manager tier, and the People team, can also open **Members**, **Access** and **Scoring & reviews** to read them; in Members a manager sees their own team. Everyone opens My settings.

On Guests, be precise: the **Guests** tab in Workspace settings, **Members**, says **Guests are people outside your company who can only see what you share with them. Inviting someone to a Space by email today creates a member account.** So the people you invite to a Space join as Members.

### Member tiers

A Member also carries a **tier**. The tier decides which manager pages they open, such as My team, Review cycles or Analytics. It does not change what they can open in Spaces and Lists, which sharing decides. An Owner or Admin sets it on the person's row in Workspace settings, **Members**.

| Tier | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Most people. |
| **Team lead**, **Manager** | People who lead others. |
| **Director**, **VP**, **Executive** | Senior leaders, who see people data across the company. |
| **People team** | The people who look after everyone's people information. |
| **Agent** | An agent account. It works as a Member, can edit what it is given, never has Full access, and never exports or invites. |

The **Agent** tier is a kind of account, not an AI teammate. AI teammates are AI helpers each person makes and chats with, and an agent account can't have them. See [AI teammates](/using/ai-teammates/overview).

Creating a Space needs an Owner, an Admin, or a Member whose tier is Team lead, Manager, Director, VP or Executive.

## Sharing work

### Share from any menu

Every Space, Folder, List and doc has **Manage access** in its `…` menu, and so do tables, forms and canvases. Below Full access the row reads **Who has access**, which shows who can open it without letting you change it. The dialog has two parts: the people it is shared with, and **General access**, which says who else can open it.

### The levels

Each kind of thing offers its own levels:

| Level | Space | Folder | List | Doc | Table | Canvas, Form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner**: Full access, plus every Restricted List in the Space | Yes | | | | | |
| **Full access**: change settings, sharing, delete and transfer | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Can edit**: add and change tasks, docs and rows | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Can edit assigned tasks**: read and discuss every task, change only tasks assigned to them or that they made | | | Yes | | | |
| **Can comment**: read and discuss, never change | | | Yes | Yes | | |
| **Can view**: read only | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | | Yes |

On a doc, **Can edit** also lets someone change who can open it, and **Full access** adds locking it, moving it to Trash and saving it as a template.

On a List, the two lowest levels work like this:

- **Can view**: read only, except tasks assigned to them or that they made, which they can change.
- **Can comment**: read and discuss every task, change none, not even their own, unless they can open the List another way.

### How sharing adds up

- **Sharing flows down.** A share on a Space reaches its Folders and Lists. A share on a Folder reaches the Lists inside it.
- **A share only adds.** Sharing something never takes away access someone already has from elsewhere.
- **Roles never climb.** Sharing a List with someone does not give them the Folder or Space above it. They see what you shared, and the path to it.
- **The path is not a place.** A Space or Folder someone sees only because something inside it was shared with them shows as a path. They can't open its other contents, and its menu has **Copy link** only.
- **Folders share on their own.** You can share a Folder without sharing its Space, and the person sees that Folder and the path to it.
- **Restricted cuts the flow.** **Restricted** on a Folder or List stops it taking access from above, so only the people listed, plus Admins, can open it. Turning it on asks first, and says who would lose access.
- **Assigning someone a task lets them open it**, and change it, even if they can't open the rest of the List, unless Can comment on that List is all the access they hold there.
- **You have Full access to what you made.**

### General access

| Thing | Who else can open it |
| --- | --- |
| Space | **Invite only** (only the people listed), **Space members** (the people listed, plus Admins), or **Everyone at** your company |
| Folder | Inherits from its Space or parent Folder, unless **Restricted** is on |
| List | **Inherits from** its Folder or Space (the default), **Restricted**, or **Everyone at** your company |
| Doc | Follows where it lives, unless **Restricted** is on. Also its public link and private link |
| Table, Form | The public link and its embed code |

Inside a Space open to everyone at the company, a share can only add rights. The dialog says so: everyone can already open everything in that Space, so to keep people to one Folder or List, set the Space to **Space members**.

### Public links

Workspace settings, **Access**, **Public links** decides whether SOPs, tasks and docs can have a public, view-only link. With it on, a task's **Public link** works as described in [A public link for one task](/using/spaces-and-tasks#a-public-link-for-one-task). With it off, no new public link can be made for an SOP, a task or a doc, and existing ones stop answering.

## People data

A person's record, under **Teams**, has an Overview and Skills for everyone in the company. The rest of it, their KRAs and KPIs, goals, reviews, assets and scores, shows only to:

- the person themselves,
- their reporting chain (their manager, that manager's manager, and up),
- the **People team**,
- people whose tier is **Director**, **VP** or **Executive**,
- Owners and Admins.

Everyone else sees the directory card: name, job title, department and manager. People pickers, such as the assignee picker in a List and the reporting manager picker, search the whole company, so you can always find a colleague by name.

### Goals

- Everyone sees Company goals.
- You see the goals you own, and the goals whose contributors include you, directly or through your department, job title or a tag.
- Everyone sees Department goals for their own department.
- A manager also sees the goals of people in their reporting chain.
- Directors, VPs, Executives, the People team and Admins see every goal.

Opening a goal you can't see shows a not found page, so a missing goal and a forbidden goal look the same from outside.

### Audience is not permission

Goals, announcements and pulse surveys carry an **audience**: everyone, some people, departments, offices, job titles or tags. The audience decides who something is aimed at, and it is worked out each time it is read, so someone who joins a department or picks up a tag is in the audience straight away. An audience never widens what anyone can open elsewhere.

### Notepad notes are yours alone

A Notepad note is private to the person who wrote it. Nobody else can open it, including Admins, and it never shows in Docs or a doc picker.

## Apps on the rail

The rail shows the hubs and apps each person can open. Owners and Admins change it in Workspace settings, **Apps & modules**: hide an app, set who sees it (**Everyone**, **Managers and up**, **People team and Admins** or **Admins only**), and change the order. Hiding an app or raising who can see it takes it off people's rails. Nothing is deleted.

## Who can do what

Workspace settings, **Access**, **Who can do what** lays out every role and tier against what they can do, read from the rules the product enforces. It is read only: you change what people can do by changing their role or tier in **Members**, or what is shared with them.
