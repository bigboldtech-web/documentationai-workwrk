---
title: Approve an AI teammate's request
description: Find what a teammate is waiting on, then approve, change or deny it.
---

When an AI teammate wants to do something other people will see, it waits on an approval card. Nothing happens until you decide.

## Find what is waiting

- **In the chat** where you asked. The card reads **Waiting for you**.
- **On the AI teammates page**, the **Waiting for you** tab lists only the teammates with something waiting, with a count.
- **In the AI sidebar**, the **AI teammates** row shows how many requests wait for you.
- **In your Inbox**, for requests a teammate made while you weren't in the chat: from a routine, an answer in Talk, or an automation. The row reads, for example, **Chief of Staff is waiting for your approval**.

## Decide

<Steps>
<Step title="Read the card" icon="eye">
It shows what will happen, the exact text that will be posted or added, who it reaches, and the date it waits until.
</Step>
<Step title="Change it, if you want" icon="pencil">
Choose **Edit** to change the one field the card allows, such as a task's title or a message, then **Approve with changes**.
</Step>
<Step title="Approve or deny" icon="check">
Choose **Approve** to run it now, or **Deny** to stop it. Nothing happens on a deny.
</Step>
</Steps>

When one answer asked for several things, the card lists them with tick boxes. Untick any you want to hold back, then choose **Approve** or **Deny** with the number. An invitation always has its own card.

From the Inbox, open the row: the same card opens right there, so you can decide without leaving the Inbox.

## Good to know

- What runs is exactly what the card shows.
- A request nobody answers expires after 7 days. Ask the teammate again if you still want it.
- When you approve in the chat, the teammate carries on from where it stopped. That uses one AI question. Denying uses none.
- **Approve and don't ask again** approves it and stops the teammate asking you for this kind of action again, where that is allowed. See [Let a teammate act without asking](/help-center/guides/let-a-teammate-act-without-asking).

More in [Approvals and Don't ask](/using/ai-teammates/approvals).
