---
title: Can my admins read my chats with an AI teammate?
description: Who can see your AI teammate chats, memories and what a teammate did for you.
---

No. Your chat with a teammate is yours. That is true for a teammate that is just yours and for one the whole workspace uses: everyone has their own chat, and nobody reads anyone else's, including Owners and Admins.

What other people can see:

- **What a teammate did.** Anything a teammate does, such as creating a task or posting in Talk, is done in your name, so people see it where it happened, as they would if you did it. The workspace's audit log records it as the teammate working for you, for example **Chief of Staff (for Priya Shah): Created task "Call Acme"**.
- **Answers in Talk.** When you ask a teammate in Talk, its answer is posted in the conversation for the people who were in it.
- **Automation answers.** When an automation you made asks a teammate, Owners and Admins can read the teammate's answer in that automation's runs, as you can.
- **Shared memories.** On a workspace teammate, memories its Owners and Admins add for everyone are seen by everyone. Your own memories are seen by nobody else.
- **Usage.** A teammate's **Activity** tab shows how many AI questions it used this month, as a number.

You can download your own chats with your other records. See [How do I download a copy of my data?](/help-center/guides/download-my-data)
