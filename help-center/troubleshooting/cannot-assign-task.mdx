---
title: I can't assign a task to someone
description: The four real reasons a person is missing from the assignee picker or the assignment is refused, and how to fix each one.
---

There are two different failures hiding behind "I can't assign this task": the person you want is not in the picker, or the picker works but the save is refused. Work through these in order.

## 1. The person is not in the picker

The assignee picker on a List offers the people who can already reach that List: its members, the members of its Folder and Space, everyone when the List is open to the whole company, and Admins. Someone who cannot open the List is not offered, because assigning them would hand them a task they could not find.

**How to fix it.** Ask someone with Full access on the List or its Space to add the person in **Manage access**, then assign them. Assigning a task to someone also lets them open that task, even if the rest of the List stays closed to them.

<Callout kind="tip">
  Typing in the picker searches the same set, so searching harder will not surface someone who cannot reach the List. If a name never appears no matter what you type, this is your answer.
</Callout>

## 2. You can only read the List, not edit it

Seeing a List is a lower bar than changing what is in it. Changing a task, including its assignees, needs:

- **Can edit** or **Full access** on the List, from the List itself or from its Folder or Space, or
- the task to be assigned to you, or made by you, when you hold **Can view** or **Can edit assigned tasks**.

**Can comment** held as your only access to the List lets you discuss every task and change none.

**How to fix it.** Ask someone with Full access to open the List's `…` menu, choose **Manage access**, and raise your level to **Can edit**.

## 3. You cannot see the List at all

If the task is not where a colleague says it is, you may not be able to open its container:

- A Space that is **Invite only** or for **Space members** shows only to the people listed, plus Admins.
- A **Restricted** Folder or List inside a Space you can see is limited to the people listed, plus Admins.

**How to fix it.** Ask to be added to the Space, Folder or List. See [Why can't I see this?](/help-center/faq/permissions-and-visibility) for the full rules.

## 4. The task is archived or deleted

Archived tasks are left out of a List's rows, so a task that "disappeared" has often been archived rather than deleted. You cannot assign what you cannot reach.

**How to fix it.** Open **Trash** in the Work sidebar. Deleted tasks are on the **Deleted** tab and archived ones on the **Archived** tab; both can be restored by the people who can edit them.

## Still stuck?

Collect these three facts before you ask your admin, because they are exactly what the admin needs:

1. The Space and List the task lives in, and whether the List or its Folder is **Restricted**.
2. What **Manage access** on the List says your level is.
3. Whether the person you are trying to assign can open the List.
