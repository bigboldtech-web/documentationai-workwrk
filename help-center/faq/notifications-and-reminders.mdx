---
title: Notifications and reminders
description: What creates a notification, where it lands, how reminders fire whether or not the app is open, and which emails WorkwrK really sends.
---

WorkwrK has two separate systems that people often confuse. **Notifications** are things the product tells you about because someone else acted. **Reminders** are alarms you set for yourself.

## What creates a notification

These are the events that write a notification today:

| Event | Who gets it |
| --- | --- |
| A task is created for you, or handed to you | The new owner |
| A task you are assigned to changes status | The owner, plus everyone who watches the task or commented on it |
| Someone comments on a task | The assignees, and everyone who watches the task |
| Someone mentions you in a task comment or a doc | The person mentioned |
| A task assigned to you is due today | The assignee, once a day in the morning |
| An SOP is assigned to you | Each assignee. The notification links to **My SOPs** |
| A goal is created with you as its owner | The owner, when someone else created it |
| A teammate gives you kudos | The recipient |
| An AI teammate is waiting for your approval | You, when the request came from a routine, Talk or an automation |
| One of your reminders fires | You |

Two rules apply to all of them: you are never notified about your own action, and your own settings are checked before the notification is written.

<Callout kind="alert">
  The **Overdue** switch is in your settings, but the daily overdue notification is not sent yet. If you need a nudge after a due date passes, set a reminder on the task, which is a separate thing described below.
</Callout>

## Where notifications land

- **The bell** in the top right shows recent notifications with an unread badge. Notifications can also be mirrored as desktop alerts for this browser, which you allow from the **Desktop** tab in My settings, **Notifications**.
- **The Inbox** in the Work sidebar is the full list, with tabs for **Primary**, **Other**, **Mentions**, **Snoozed** and **Cleared**. Open a row and its task, doc or approval card opens beside the list.

## Reminders

A reminder is an alarm with a time. There are two ways to set one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="A standalone reminder" icon="alarm-clock">
    Use the **Reminder** quick tool in the top bar, or **Reminder** in the Planner sidebar's **+** menu. Give it a title, then pick a time or use the quick chips. Tick **Also email me** if you want an email as well as the in-app alert. Press **Set reminder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A reminder on a task" icon="calendar-clock">
    Open the task and use its date planner. Alongside **Date** and **Repeat** there is a **Reminder** tab, where reminders can be set relative to the due date or at a custom time. These are real reminders attached to the task, so firing one links straight back to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the **Reminder** tool is not in your top bar, open your avatar menu and pin it under **Personal tools**.

### How a reminder fires

Two mechanisms cover the two cases, and they cannot double-fire because each due reminder is claimed exactly once:

- **While the app is open**, the browser checks for due reminders on load and then every minute.
- **While the app is closed**, a job on the server fires anything due every few minutes, so the reminder is waiting in the bell when you come back.

A fired reminder appears as a persistent card in the bottom right. It stays until you act on it. Each card offers **Snooze** with 10 minutes, 1 hour and tomorrow morning, plus **Dismiss**, and **Open** when the reminder belongs to a task.

The reminders bell keeps the whole lifecycle: reminders that already fired and have not been acted on, then Overdue, Today and Upcoming, then a read-only history of recently dismissed ones.

Opening a task from its reminder counts as acting on it, so the card disappears. If you want to look at the task but keep the alarm, snooze it first.

## Controlling what you get

Everything is per person, in My settings, **Notifications**, on three tabs. Changes save automatically.

**Inbox** decides what lands in your Inbox and the bell. A **Notification preset** picks a starting point: **Default**, **Focused** (only what is aimed at you: tasks assigned to you, mentions and your overdue tasks) or **Custom**. The rows are:

- **Tell me about my work**: Tasks assigned to me, Status changes on my tasks, Status changes on tasks I follow, Due soon, Overdue.
- **Tell me about people**: Mentions of me, Comments on my work, Comments on tasks I follow, Kudos I receive.
- **Talk and announcements**.
- **How the Inbox behaves**: which tab the Inbox opens on.
- **Muted items**: Spaces, Folders or Lists you muted. Tasks assigned to you and mentions still reach you.

Switching a row off stops that notification being created at all. "Follow" means the task's **Watch** button, or having commented on it.

**Email** has a master switch, **Send me email**, that governs **Tasks assigned to me by email** and **Kudos by email**. **Work updates by email** (**KRA and KPI updates**, **Review reminders** and **SOP updates**) send whether or not Send me email is on. Mentions, comments, status changes, due reminders and a daily digest by email are listed under **Coming soon**.

**Desktop** allows alerts for this browser. Each browser and device asks for permission separately.

<Callout kind="alert">
  A row under **Coming soon** is not a toggle you forgot to switch on. It means WorkwrK does not send that email yet, so it is shown honestly instead of as a control that would do nothing.
</Callout>

The avatar menu has the quick versions: **Do not disturb**, and **Mute notifications** for an hour, until tomorrow, or until you turn it back on.

## When you expected a notification and got nothing

1. Check the matching row in My settings, **Notifications**, **Inbox**. A switched-off row stops the notification being created at all, and a muted Space, Folder or List stops status, comment and due date updates from it.
2. Confirm you were not the one who acted. Your own changes never notify you.
3. For emails, check whether that row is under **Coming soon**, and whether **Send me email** is on. Work updates by email ignore the master switch.
4. For a reminder, check the reminders bell. A reminder that fired while you were away is not lost: it comes back as a card the next time you open the app, and until you act on it the bell keeps it in its fired list.
