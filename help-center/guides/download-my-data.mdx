---
title: Download a copy of your data
description: Download your own records from WorkwrK as a file.
---

Open My settings, then **Profile**. Under **Your data**, choose **Download**. A JSON file downloads to your device.

The file holds your personal records, including:

- your profile and your consents,
- your notifications and your activity, the newest 10,000 of each,
- your KPI records, KRAs, reviews you were the subject of or wrote, feedback, check-ins, meetings, action items, kudos and ideas,
- your AI teammate chats, the requests teammates asked you to approve, what they remember about you, your routines and your approval choices.

Very long histories are cut to fit, and the file says how many items it left out.

Only you can download this file. For a copy of the whole workspace, an Owner or Admin uses Workspace settings, **Data**, **Export**.
