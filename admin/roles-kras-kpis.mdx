---
title: Roles, KRAs and KPIs
description: Build a job title, hang weighted KRAs off it, add KPI gauges, and understand exactly what a person inherits when you give them that role.
---

WorkwrK's alignment chain runs job title, then KRA, then KPI, then person. A **role** here is a job title. A KRA is a permanent area of responsibility inside that job title. A KPI is a running gauge underneath a KRA. A person holds one job title and inherits its KRAs.

You build the template once on the job title. Everyone who holds it inherits it.

## Create a job title

1. Go to **Teams**, **Job titles**.
2. Choose **New job title**.
3. Type the title and confirm.

The job title's page opens, which is where the real setup happens. Fill in its **Details** before you move on: the description, the department and the seniority.

Job titles are unique per workspace.

The Job titles page groups titles by seniority and shows headcount per title.

A job title can be renamed in its Details card, which saves as you type. Its **...** menu has **Duplicate as new job title** (the copy has no KRAs: attach the ones it needs) and **Delete job title**, which shows only when nobody holds the title and it defines no KRAs.

## The job title page

Opening a job title gives you two tabs.

**Overview** carries five cards:

- **Details**: the title, a description of what the job title is for and what it must ensure, the department, the seniority, and a count of people holding the title. See [Departments, roles and tiers](/admin/functions-and-levels).
- **Ownership boundary**: three columns, Owns, Can request, Cannot touch. One concept, one owner. Adding an owned area creates it; the Can request and Cannot touch columns link the job title to areas owned elsewhere. **Raise a request to the owner** on a Can request row asks what you need changed or decided, and sends it to everyone who holds the owning job title, in their Inbox.
- **KRAs & KPIs**: the template. Covered below.
- **Escalation thresholds**: label, trigger, value and unit, for example "Unclaimed → nudge" at 45 min. These are stored on the job title. They are configuration for automation, not something that fires on its own today.
- **SOPs**: read only. It lists every SOP attached to this job title's KRAs, with the KRA name and the SOP status. You attach a SOP to a KRA from the SOPs module, not from here.

**Instances** is the job title by scope view. An instance is this job title held in a particular scope, for example "Dispatch Executive, Pool 1". Each instance can carry a person, and **Apply definition** seeds that person from this job title's templates.

## Add KRAs and set weights

On the **KRAs & KPIs** card, click **Add KRA**. The dialog asks for:

- **Job title**: locked to the one you are on.
- **Name**, for example "Pipeline generation".
- **Description**, optional: what outcome this area owns.
- **Weight %**, 0 to 100.

The weight is the share of the job title this area carries. Every holder inherits it as their starting weight.

The card shows the sum of this job title's KRA weights and says **Aim for 100%**.

Be clear on what that indicator is and is not. It is a visual aid. Each individual weight is checked to be between 0 and 100, but nothing enforces that a job title's KRAs sum to 100. You can save a title at 60% or at 140%. Nothing blocks it, so check the number yourself before you walk away.

To reuse an existing KRA, use the **Attach existing** picker next to the Add KRA button. It lists KRAs not already on this job title and re homes the one you pick.

### The KRA row menu

Each KRA heading has a "…" menu:

- **Edit KRA**: name, description, weight, and which job title it belongs to.
- **Add KPI**: opens the KPI dialog for that KRA.
- **Detach from job title**: the KRA moves to the "Needs a job title" list on the KRA and KPI page. People already assigned keep their assignment and history. New holders of this title stop inheriting it until it is re attached.
- **Delete KRA**: removes everyone's assignment to it and detaches its KPI gauges from the job title. Recorded readings on those gauges survive. This cannot be undone.

## Add KPIs under a KRA

Click **Add KPI** on a KRA, either from the row menu or the button at the bottom of the KRA block. The dialog collects:

- **Name**, for example "Qualified demos booked", and an optional definition: what exactly is counted, and where does the number come from?
- **Direction of good**: Higher is better, Lower is better, or Hold the line.
- **Measurement**: Quantitative, a number, or Qualitative, a 1 to 5 rating with labelled anchors.
- **Recorded**: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or annually. The recorder converts a target set at one cadence into the target for the period being recorded.
- **Healthy line** and **Baseline today**, both optional numbers.
- **Unit**, free text: %, currency, "deals".
- **Shared gauge** switch.
- **North-star gauge** switch.

Leave the healthy line blank until you actually have a baseline. The gauge then reads "no baseline yet" instead of showing an invented number. That is deliberate: WorkwrK never fabricates a target for you.

**Owned vs Shared** is the accountability switch. Owned means the job title controls the number, and it is graded. Shared means the job title influences the number, and it is reviewed but not graded. The row shows the badge either way.

North star gauges sort to the top of the KRA and show a star.

A KPI is a permanent running gauge. It has no deadline and no per person number on this page. People record their own readings against it, and those readings feed reviews and can drive goal key results.

## What happens when you give someone the job title

This is the part worth reading twice, because inheritance fires at specific moments.

**When you change a person's job title.** Setting a person's job title on their record seeds their KRAs from that title's templates. Each starts at that KRA's job title weight. SOPs sitting under those KRAs are assigned at the same time. Only published SOPs are seeded, so anything not published reaches nobody. Re running the seed never duplicates and never disturbs a weight you already adjusted.

**When you add a KRA to a job title.** Everyone who already holds that title and is active inherits the new KRA immediately, at the title's weight. Re homing an existing KRA onto a job title does the same. This is what stops a title and its holders drifting apart.

**When someone accepts an invitation.** If the invitation carried a job title, its KRAs and published SOPs are assigned when they join, at the title's weights. See [People and invites](/admin/people-and-invites).

**What does not happen.** Changing a KRA's weight later does not rewrite the weight of people already assigned. Existing per person weights stay exactly where they are. Only new seeds pick up the new default. If you rebalance a job title and want current holders updated, you have to adjust them per person, or seed again.

## Seed drift and the Seed button

At the bottom of the KRAs & KPIs card there is a **People with this job title** row, one chip per holder.

Seeding fires at specific moments, and it can miss people. The fan out when you add a KRA to a job title only covers holders whose status is Active, so anyone inactive or on leave at that moment is skipped. Holders who predate a template, and any seed that failed quietly, drift the same way. The page detects all of it.

A drifted holder's chip turns amber and reads **missing N of M**, meaning they are missing N of this job title's M template KRAs. Next to it is a **Seed** button.

Clicking **Seed** backfills only the missing KRAs from this job title's templates, at the title's weights, plus any published SOPs under those KRAs. It does not touch KRAs the person already has, including weights you have overridden.

The same action exists as **Apply definition** on the Instances tab, and as **Seed from job title** inside the person's Manage alignment dialog.

## Per person weight overrides

The job title sets the starting weights. Real people drift from the template, and that is fine.

1. Go to **Teams**, **Directory** and open the person.
2. On the **KRAs & KPIs** tab, open **Manage alignment**.

The dialog lists their KRAs with an editable weight box on each row, their assigned SOPs, and a **Seed from job title** button in the header.

- Edit a weight inline. It commits on blur or Enter, and Escape reverts.
- The header shows the person's running total, in red above 100%.
- The server bounds each individual weight to between 1 and 100, and that bound is a hard reject. The person's **total** across active assignments is no longer a hard cap. A save that pushes the total over 100 still goes through, and the server hands back an advisory warning with the running total instead of refusing. This is deliberate: a hard reject would trap a person already over budget (from role-seed defaults or legacy data) so they could never save the very edit that rebalances them. Watch the red total and trim another KRA to get back under 100.

There is no 100% ceiling enforced anywhere, for a person or a job title. A title can be built to 140%, and a person can end up over 100%; in both cases nothing blocks the save, and the only signal is the running total turning red. Check it yourself before you walk away.

The dialog also lets you add a KRA outside the person's job title template (the picker flags KRAs with no job title), remove a KRA, and add or remove SOP assignments.

## Where else this lives

**Teams**, **KRAs & KPIs** is a job title first index. It lists every title with its headcount and KRA and KPI counts, and clicking one opens the same job title page. It also surfaces a **Needs a job title** section for KRAs that belong to no title, so an admin can attach them. Orphan KRAs are never deleted or auto assigned.

## Who can do what

Every Member can read the Job titles, KRAs & KPIs and Departments pages. Changing them is for Owners and Admins, and for whoever Workspace settings, **Access** allows: **Who can do what** lays it out. See [Settings](/admin/settings).
