---
title: How do I invite my team?
description: The invite flow, what an invitation carries, the domain and seat rules, and what a new person finds waiting when they join.
---

An invitation in WorkwrK can carry a job title, and the job title is the definition: its KRAs, KPIs and SOPs attach automatically when the person joins. So the dialog asks for a little more than an address, and nothing else needs to exist first.

## Before you start

You need to be allowed to invite people: Owners and Admins are, and so are managers, who see their own team in Members. Every invitation also needs:

- **An allowed email domain.** The workspace's own domain is always allowed. Owners and Admins add others under **Invite rules** in Workspace settings, **Members**, **Pending invites**.
- **A free seat.** Every open invitation takes a seat. When the workspace is full, the invitation is refused. See [Plans and billing](/admin/plans-and-billing).

## Sending the invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Invite people" icon="user-plus">
    Choose **Invite** on the Directory under **Teams**, or in Workspace settings, **Members**. Both open the same dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the email addresses" icon="mail">
    Type or paste into **Email addresses**. Commas, spaces and Enter all turn an address into a chip. Anything that is not a valid address is flagged rather than silently dropped. One invitation is sent per address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the access level" icon="shield-check">
    **Access level** is the role and tier the person starts with. The Owner role is never offered. **This is an agent account** makes an account for a bot or service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place them" icon="network">
    **Department**, **Role** and **Reporting manager** are optional, but worth filling in. The reporting manager puts the person inside a manager's chain, which drives what each of them can see later. The **Role** is what makes their KRAs, KPIs and published SOPs attach when they join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a personal message and send" icon="send">
    **Personal message** is optional and is quoted inside the invitation email. Then press **Send invite**. Addresses that fail, for example because that person is already invited, stay in the field so you can fix and retry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the invitee does

1. They receive an invitation email with a link to your workspace.
2. The link opens the **Join** page, which shows what they are joining. Someone new chooses a name and a password; someone who already has a WorkwrK account logs in, then joins.
3. They land in the workspace with the access level, department, job title and manager the invitation carried. If it carried a job title, its KRAs and published SOPs are already waiting on their record and under **My SOPs**.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Invitations expire after the number of days set in **Invite rules** (7 by default). An expired invitation shows **Ask for a new invitation** on the Join page. In Workspace settings, **Members**, **Pending invites** shows each invitation as **Pending** or **Expired**, and lets you resend or revoke it.
</Callout>

## Managing invitations after sending

Workspace settings, **Members**, **Pending invites** lists every invitation not yet accepted, with its email, role, whether it is to the workspace or to a Space, when it expires, and its status. A resend gives it a new link and a fresh expiry. Revoke cancels it.

## Inviting someone into a single Space

A Space's **Manage access** dialog can also invite by email. That invitation carries the Space and drops the person straight into it when they accept. They join the workspace as a Member.

## Inviting many people at once

Workspace settings, **Data**, **Import**, **People from a CSV** checks each row first, and everyone ready gets an invitation. Nobody joins until they accept, and everyone joins as a Member.
