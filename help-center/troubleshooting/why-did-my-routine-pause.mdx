---
title: Why did my routine pause?
description: What each reason means, and how to start the routine again.
---

A routine pauses by itself when something stops it from running as you. You get an Inbox row, such as **Chief of Staff paused a routine**, and a line in your chat with the teammate that starts **Routine paused:** and gives the reason.

Once the reason is fixed, open the teammate's **Settings**, then **Routines**, and choose **Resume** in the routine's menu. Only you can resume your routines.

| The reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| This workspace has used all its AI questions. Resume it after the plan changes. | Ask an Owner or Admin to change the plan in Workspace settings, **Plan & billing**. |
| The person it works for has used the free AI questions one person gets across free workspaces. Resume it after the plan changes. | On the free Starter plan you have a limit of your own. The plan needs to change. |
| Its teammate's settings changed since this routine was set up or last resumed. | Someone who manages the teammate changed its name, job, instructions, tools, approval rules or model. Check the teammate, then resume. |
| This teammate was removed. | Ask whoever manages it to add it back, or delete the routine. |
| The person it works for can no longer use this teammate. | You lost access to it. Ask whoever manages it. |
| AI isn't available to the person it works for. | AI is off for you or for the workspace. Ask an Owner or Admin. |
| Its schedule names no time in the next year. | Edit the routine and choose when it runs. |

## Skipped, not paused

Some runs are skipped while the routine stays on. It runs again at its next time:

- **This teammate is paused.** Whoever manages it can turn it on again.
- **This teammate has used its AI questions for the month.** It answers again on the 1st (UTC), or whoever manages it can raise its monthly limit.
- **Skipped: the scheduler reached it more than three hours late.**
