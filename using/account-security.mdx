Everything on this page is in **My settings**, **Security**. Open My settings from your avatar menu in the top bar.

## How you log in

### Change your password

Next to **Password**, choose **Change password**. The dialog shows your workspace's password rules. The row under **Password** says when you last changed it.

If you forgot your password, choose **Forgot your password?** on the log in page. The reset link in the email works once, and expires.

### Two step verification

Two step verification asks for a 6 digit code from an authenticator app each time you log in, after your password.

<Steps>
<Step title="Turn it on" icon="shield-check">
Next to **Two step verification**, choose **Turn on**.
</Step>
<Step title="Scan the code" icon="qr-code">
Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy or any authenticator app. If you can't scan it, choose **Can't scan? Enter this key** and type the key into the app.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter a code" icon="hash">
Type the 6 digit code the app shows. Codes change every 30 seconds.
</Step>
<Step title="Save your backup codes" icon="key">
You get 8 backup codes. They are shown once. Keep them somewhere safe: each one lets you log in once if you lose your phone.
</Step>
</Steps>

Once it is on, the menu beside **Two step verification** has **Show backup codes**, **Get new backup codes** and **Turn off**. The **Backup codes** row shows how many of the 8 are unused, with **Get new codes**.

If your workspace requires two step verification for your role, **Turn off** is not offered.

Once two step verification is on for you, or your workspace requires it for your role, you log in with your email, password and code. Logging in with Google is refused with **Signing in to this account needs two step verification.** If you have never set a password, choose **Forgot your password?** on the log in page to set one.

### Email verified

If your email is not verified, choose **Send verification email**, then open the link in the email.

## Log out everywhere

Under **Where you are logged in**, **Log out everywhere** ends every session, on every device, including this one. Use it if you lost a device or think someone else has your password. After it, every device has to log in again. A device that was open shows **You were logged out**, with the line **You were logged out on every device. Log in again to keep working.**

## Staying logged in

WorkwrK logs you out after a while without activity: 12 hours unless your workspace's sign-in policy sets a shorter time. Before it happens you get a warning. If your session ends, a dialog asks you to log in again and brings you back to the page you were on.

Too many wrong passwords in a row stop logging in for a while. The log in page says how many minutes to wait. Wait, then try again, or reset your password.

## Recent security activity

**Recent security activity** lists your last 20 security events in plain words: log ins and log outs, password changes and resets, two step verification turned on or off, new backup codes, and Log out everywhere. If you see something you didn't do, change your password and choose **Log out everywhere**.

## Presence

The **Presence** card sets what teammates see on your avatar dot. **Show me as** picks the status, and **Clear after** sets when it goes back. The avatar menu's **Set status…** changes the same status.

## For Owners and Admins

Owners and Admins see a **Workspace sign-in policy** link on this page. It opens Workspace settings, **Security**, **Sign-in policy**, where the workspace's password rules, two step verification requirements, session length and failed sign-in lockout are set. See [Settings](/admin/settings).
