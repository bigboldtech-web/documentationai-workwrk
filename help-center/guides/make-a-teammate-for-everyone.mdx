Only an Owner or Admin can make a teammate for the whole workspace.

<Steps>
<Step title="Start a new teammate" icon="plus">
On the AI teammates page, choose **New**, then **New teammate**.
</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the form" icon="pencil">
Pick a template or **Start from scratch**, and fill in the form.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose who can use it" icon="users">
Under **Who can use it**, choose **Everyone in the workspace**.
</Step>
<Step title="Create it" icon="check">
Choose **Create teammate**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## How a workspace teammate works

- Everyone gets their own chat with it. Nobody reads anyone else's chat.
- It works as whoever is using it, with that person's access. It never sees more than they can.
- Owners and Admins manage it: its name, instructions, tools, monthly limit, and shared memories for everyone.
- Owners and Admins can set a tool to **Ask everyone first**. Everyone else chooses their own **Ask me first** or **Don't ask** within that.
- Each person's memories and routines with it are their own.
- If an Owner or Admin changes it, everyone's routines with it pause until each person checks it and resumes them.

Your plan sets how many workspace teammates you can have: 3 on Starter, 30 on Growth and 100 on Scale.
