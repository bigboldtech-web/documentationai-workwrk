---
title: Let a teammate act without asking
description: Choose Don't ask for a tool, or approve a card and don't ask again.
---

## For a whole tool

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the teammate's settings" icon="settings">
In its chat header, choose **Settings**, then **Tools and approvals**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Don't ask" icon="toggle-right">
Beside the tool, change **Ask me first** to **Don't ask**.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also choose it beside each tool when you make a teammate.

## From an approval card

Choose **Approve and don't ask again**. It approves this request and stops the teammate asking you for the same kind of action. For a post in Talk, the button names the conversation, for example **Approve and don't ask again in #general**, and covers that conversation only.

## What Don't ask never covers

- **Invite people** always asks.
- **Post in Talk** can only be let go one conversation at a time, from a card.
- A tool-wide **Don't ask** covers only your own work. When the same tool would reach other people, such as a task for someone else, it keeps asking until you choose **Approve and don't ask again** on one of those cards.
- When the teammate answers in Talk, runs in an automation, or is asked by another of your teammates, it asks first whatever you chose.
- On a workspace teammate, if an Owner or Admin chose **Ask everyone first** for a tool, it asks you first.

## Change your mind

On **Tools and approvals**, change the tool back to **Ask me first**. Choices you made from cards are listed under the tool, each with **Remove**.

Your choices apply only to you. On a workspace teammate, everyone makes their own.
