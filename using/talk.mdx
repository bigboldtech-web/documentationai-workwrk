Talk is WorkwrK's chat: channels for a topic or a team, direct messages and small groups, threads, reactions, mentions, files and calls, in the same workspace as your tasks. Open it from **Talk** on the rail.

<Callout kind="info">
  Talk is a premium module, included from the Growth plan. An Owner or Admin turns it on in Workspace settings, **Apps & modules**. While it is off, Talk is not on the rail. See [Why can't I turn on Talk or Tables?](/help-center/troubleshooting/turn-on-talk-or-tables)
</Callout>

## Talk home

The Talk sidebar starts with three views of Talk home:

- **Unread**: every conversation with something you have not read. **Mark all as read** clears them all. When nothing is waiting it says **You're all caught up**.
- **Threads**: the threads you are in, with their reply counts.
- **Mentions**: the messages where someone mentioned you.

Pick a row and the conversation opens in the pane beside the list. **Open full page** opens it on its own page.

Under the views, the sidebar lists your conversations: **Starred** first when you have starred any, then **Channels**, then **Direct messages**. A dot marks a conversation with unread messages, and a number counts unread mentions. Use the sidebar search box to find a conversation.

## Start a conversation

- **New message** opens a people picker. Pick one person for a direct message, or several for a group, then **Start chat** or **Start group**.
- **New channel** (the arrow beside New message) makes a channel. Give it a name and, if you like, a topic (**What is this channel for?**). Then choose:
  - leave **Restricted: only people you add** off, and the channel is **Findable by everyone at this company. Anyone can open it and join.**
  - turn it on, and **Nobody else can find or open it, workspace admins included. You will add people next.**
- **Browse channels**, at the foot of the Channels section, lists the channels you can join. Choose **Join** on one.

Guests can't make channels.

## Write and read messages

Type in the message box and press Enter to send.

- **Format**: **Show formatting** opens bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, links, bulleted and numbered lists, quotes, code and code blocks.
- **Mention someone**: type @ and pick a person. They are told in their Inbox and see it under **Mentions**.
- **Attach files** adds files to the message.
- **Emoji** adds an emoji to your text.

Hover a message for **React**, **Reply in thread** and **More message actions**. You can edit and remove your own messages. A message that did not send stays in the conversation with **Retry**.

**Reply in thread** opens the thread in a panel beside the conversation, so a side discussion does not fill the channel.

## Manage a conversation

The **...** button in a conversation's header (**Conversation options**) has **Star** or **Remove star**, the notification choice, **Copy link**, and the management rows your access allows.

- **Notifications**: **All messages**, **Mentions only** or **Muted**. Channels start at Mentions only, direct messages and groups at All messages.
- **Details** opens the panel with the topic (**Edit topic**), the members, the files (**Show all files**), **Search this conversation**, and, for a channel's managers, the **Restricted** and **Findable** switches.
- **Add people** adds members.
- **Leave channel** or **Leave this group?** takes you out. The conversation and its history stay for everyone else.
- **Close conversation** takes a direct message out of your sidebar. Its history is kept.

Who can do what in a channel:

- A member posts, reacts, attaches files, starts calls and adds people.
- The person who made the channel, and Owners and Admins on a channel that is not restricted, can also rename it, change its topic and members, change Restricted and Findable, and archive it.
- A restricted channel has no admin read-around: an Owner or Admin sees its name in **Browse channels** and can archive it there, but never reads its messages.

### Archive and restore a channel

Owners and Admins see an **All channels** tab in **Browse channels**, with **Archive channel** and **Restore channel** on each row. An archived channel's history stays: **Channel archived. Its history stays.** Everyone can still read it, and nobody can post until it is restored. The company channel, #general, can't be archived.

## Calls

Every conversation can hold a call. In the header, choose **Call** to start a video call, or the arrow beside it (**Call options**) for **Video call** or **Audio call**. An audio call is the quick way to huddle with the people in a conversation.

- While a call runs, the conversation's row in the sidebar shows it as live, with how many people are on it.
- When someone calls you, an incoming call card rings, with **Join with video**.

### Invite someone from outside

People outside the workspace can join a conversation's calls through its guest link. No account is needed.

<Steps>
<Step title="Copy the guest link" icon="link">
In the header, open **Call options**, then **Copy guest link**. The Details panel also shows it as **Guest link**: **Anyone with this link can join this conversation's calls until** a date and time, about a day after you opened the conversation.
</Step>
<Step title="Send it" icon="send">
Send the link to your guest by email or any other way.
</Step>
<Step title="Your guest joins" icon="video">
They open the link, type **Your name**, and choose **Join call**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Members of a conversation can copy its guest link. Its managers also see **Reset guest link**, which ends every earlier link at once: **Guest link reset. Every earlier link is dead.** A link that has run out says **This link has expired**, and a link for an archived conversation says **This call link no longer works**.

<Callout kind="alert">
  Calls run on a video service your workspace's server must have set up. Until it is, choosing Call says **Calls aren't set up for this workspace**, and a guest link says **Calls aren't available right now.** Messages work either way.
</Callout>

## AI teammates in Talk

In a private channel, a group or a direct message, you can type @ and pick one of your AI teammates to answer there. See [Teammates in Talk and Automations](/using/ai-teammates/talk-and-automations).

## Announcements

People who may post announcements see **New announcement** in Talk home's **...** menu. It opens the announcement composer, where **Who sees this** picks the audience.
