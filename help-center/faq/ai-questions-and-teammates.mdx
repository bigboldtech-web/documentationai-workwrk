---
title: How many AI questions does an AI teammate use?
description: What counts as an AI question, and how to keep a teammate within a limit.
---

Your workspace's plan includes a total number of AI questions: 50 on Starter, 500 on Growth and 2,000 on Scale. It is a total, not a monthly allowance. Workspace settings, **Plan & billing**, shows how many are used.

A teammate uses one question for each:

- message you send it, however many steps it takes to answer,
- time it carries on in the chat after you approve something,
- teammate that answers in a group chat,
- routine run, including **Run now** and **Practice run**,
- message sent with **Practice run** on,
- time it asks another of your teammates,
- answer in Talk,
- run of an automation step that asks it.

Denying a request, deciding from the Inbox, and changing its settings or memories use none. A request that fails before the AI answers is not counted.

## Keep a teammate within a limit

Whoever manages a teammate can set a **Monthly limit** on its **Instructions** tab: the most questions it may use in a calendar month, on top of the plan. The **Activity** tab's **Usage** shows how many it used this month.

To save questions, pause routines you don't need. See [How do I stop or pause a routine?](/help-center/guides/stop-a-routine)

On Starter, each person also has 50 free questions in total across every free workspace they use.

More in [Limits and plans](/using/ai-teammates/limits).
