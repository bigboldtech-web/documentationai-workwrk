---
title: Departments, roles and tiers
description: What a department, a job title, a workspace role and a member tier each mean in WorkwrK, where each one lives, and what each one decides.
---

Four words in WorkwrK get confused constantly. This page settles them.

- **Department** is where a job title and its people sit in the business.
- **Job title** is the job: it carries the KRAs and SOPs.
- **Workspace role** is Owner, Admin, Member or Guest: what a person can do in the workspace as a whole.
- **Tier** is a Member's seniority: which manager pages they open.

They are stored separately, edited in different places, and do different jobs.

## Departments

### Where departments live

Under **Teams**, **Departments**, and in Workspace settings, **Structure**, on the **Departments** tab. Every Member can read the list; Owners, Admins and the people the workspace allows can change it.

The page shows a tree with headcount per department, a search box, and a Tree or Grid toggle.

### Creating and changing a department

Choose **New department** and fill in a name and, if you like, a description, a colour, a department head and a parent department. Department names are unique in a workspace.

A department can be renamed, recoloured, given a different head, moved under another department, or deleted. The server refuses to delete a department that still has people in it, so move them first. There is no merge: move the people across and delete the empty one.

### Attaching a department to a job title

A job title carries its department. Open the job title under **Teams**, **Job titles**, and set **Department** in its **Details** card.

### Attaching a department to a person

A person's department is set on their record, not on the job title. Owners, Admins and the People team change it from the person's record or from Workspace settings, **Members**. The **Invite people** dialog also takes a department, so a hire can arrive already placed. See [People and invites](/admin/people-and-invites).

## Job titles

A job title is a job, not a permission. It has a title, a description of what it is for and what it must ensure, a **Seniority**, and a department. It is where KRAs are defined, and everyone who holds it inherits those KRAs and the published SOPs under them. See [Roles, KRAs and KPIs](/admin/roles-kras-kpis).

## Workspace roles

Everyone in a workspace holds one of four roles. Owners and Admins set them on a person's row in Workspace settings, **Members**.

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Runs the company account: billing, ownership and security. Only an Owner can make someone else an Owner, and the workspace always keeps at least one. |
| **Admin** | Runs the workspace day to day: opens every Space, invites people and uses every Workspace settings page. |
| **Member** | An employee: works in the Spaces they are added to and sees what is open to the whole company. |
| **Guest** | Outside the company, sees only what is shared. |

What someone can open inside Work is decided by sharing, not by their role: a Member opens the Spaces, Folders and Lists shared with them, and whatever is open to everyone at the company. See [Visibility and permissions](/reference/visibility-and-permissions).

## Tiers

A Member also carries a tier, set on the same row in **Members**. The tier **decides which manager pages they open**, as the row's helper says, and the invite dialog offers the same choices as an access level.

| Tier | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Their own work and record. |
| **Team lead**, **Manager** | My team, Workload, Alignment, KPI reviews and Weekly reviews for their own chain, and they can create Spaces and run a review cycle for their chain. |
| **Director**, **VP**, **Executive** | The same, plus people data across the whole company. |
| **People team** | Everyone's people information, review cycles, and the Members, Structure, Access and Scoring & reviews pages. |
| **Agent** | An agent account: works as a Member, can edit what it is given, never has Full access, and never exports or invites. |

The tier does not sync with the job title's seniority. Changing a job title's seniority does not change the tiers of the people who hold it.

### Where you set each one

| What | Where | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A job title's department and seniority | Teams, Job titles, open the job title, Details | Owners, Admins and the people the workspace allows to edit job titles |
| A person's role and tier | Workspace settings, Members, the person's row | Owners and Admins; to or from Owner, an Owner only |
| A person's department, job title, office and manager | Their record, or Workspace settings, Members | Owners, Admins and the People team |
| An invited person's starting level | The Invite people dialog, Access level | Whoever sends the invitation, within what they may give |

## Who sees whose people data

A person's KRAs and KPIs, goals, reviews, assets and scores show to the person, their reporting chain, the People team, Directors and above, and Owners and Admins. Everyone else sees the directory card: name, job title, department and manager. Hiding a page is never the permission: the same rule is checked on the server when the data is read.

Two more rules:

- **Removing a person** is for Owners and Admins, and for managers over people in their own chain, other than Owners and Admins. Nobody can remove themselves.
- **A change to someone's role** takes effect on their next request, within minutes.
