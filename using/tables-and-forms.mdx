The **Tables** hub on the rail holds two things:

- **Tables**, a spreadsheet for reference data and numbers: a price book, a client list, an inventory, a budget.
- **Forms**, for collecting answers from your team or from outside, which can feed a List or a table.

The hub sidebar lists **All tables**, **All forms**, your **Favorites**, then every table and every form you can open, with **New table** and **New form** at the foot of each section.

<Callout kind="info">
  Tables is a premium module, included from the Growth plan. An Owner or Admin turns it on in Workspace settings, **Apps & modules**. Forms are part of every plan, so **All forms** shows even when Tables is off. See [Why can't I turn on Talk or Tables?](/help-center/troubleshooting/turn-on-talk-or-tables)

## Tables

### Make a table

Choose **New table** in the sidebar, or **Import a CSV…** from the hub's **+** menu. A new table starts with columns A to Z and 1,000 blank rows, like a new sheet. A table that has no columns yet offers **Start the table**.

A table can sit inside a Space, and can open in place in Work like a doc or a canvas. **File**, **Move to Space…** moves it.

### The menu bar

Every table has the menus a spreadsheet has. A row you can't use is left out.

| Menu | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **File** | New table, Make a copy, Import a CSV…, Download (CSV, formatted values, or CSV, raw values), Print, Move to Space…, Rename, About…, Move to Trash |
| **Edit** | Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Fill down, Fill right, Find and replace, Clear cells, Delete rows, Delete columns |
| **View** | Freeze rows and columns, Gridlines, Formula bar, Zoom |
| **Insert** | Row above, Row below, Column left, Column right, Function (with More functions… for the full list), Named range… |
| **Format** | Number, Text (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough), Alignment, Text colour, Fill colour, Conditional formatting…, Data validation…, Column width…, Row height…, Clear formatting |
| **Data** | Sort table, Clear sort, Filter, Column type, Protect column, Pivot table…, Ask your data…, Trash… |

Under 900 pixels wide, the six menus fold into one **Menu** button.

### Cells and formulas

Click a cell and type. Start with = to write a formula, and the formula bar shows it. **Insert**, **Function** lists common functions, and **More functions…** opens the full reference. A **Named range…** gives a range a name you can use in formulas.

**Find and replace** searches the sheet and can replace what it finds. **Undo** and **Redo** step back and forward through your changes.

### Column types

Choose **Column type** from the **Data** menu or a column's menu:

Text, Long text, Number, Currency, Percent, Date, Checkbox, Single select, Multiple select and Rating, plus, under **Show more**, Person, Link, Attachment, Formula, Link to another table, Lookup and Rollup.

- **Link to another table** points a column at rows in another table.
- **Lookup** shows a column from the linked row.
- **Rollup** adds up, counts or averages values from the linked rows.

Changing a column's type says first how many cells it changes.

### Formatting, validation and protection

- **Conditional formatting…** colours cells by rule: **Single color**, **Color scale**, **Data bar** or **Icon set**.
- **Data validation…** limits what a column accepts.
- **Protect column** stops a column being changed. **Unprotect column** lifts it.

### Sort, filter and pivot

- **Sort table** sorts by the column you are in. **Clear sort** puts it back.
- **Filter** shows or hides the table's filters.
- **Pivot table…** groups rows by one column, can split them across another, and totals a value. Switch the result between **Table** and **Chart** (bar, line or pie). The result is worked out from the rows each time; **Insert as a new table** keeps it as a table of its own.

### Ask your data

Where AI is on for your workspace, **Data**, **Ask your data…** answers questions about the table in plain words, such as **Summarise this table**. A long table is answered from its first 300 rows, and the answer says so.

### Deleted rows

Rows you delete go to the table's own trash: **Data**, **Trash…**. A deleted table goes to the workspace Trash.

### Share a table

Use **Share** in the table's header. A table can be shared with **Full access** or **Can edit**. The person who made it holds Full access, and so do the workspace's Owners and Admins.

The same dialog has the table's **Public link**. Turning it on asks first: anyone with the link can see every row and column, without signing in. You can turn it off at any time. While it is on, the dialog also gives you code to embed the table on another page.

## Forms

### Make a form

Choose **New form** in the Tables sidebar. The form opens with three tabs: **Build**, **Settings** and **Responses**. There is no Save button: every change saves as you go.

### Build

- Give the form a title and a description.
- **+ Add a field** adds a question. Each field has a label, help text, options where the type needs them, and a **Required** switch.
- **Preview** shows the form as people will see it.

### Where answers go

The **Goes to** card sends each response somewhere:

- to a **List**, where each response becomes a task, or
- to a **table**, where each response becomes a row, or both, or **Nowhere yet**.

Once a destination is set, the field mapping card matches each question to a field of the List or a column of the table. It matches by name to start, and **Don't send** keeps an answer on the Responses tab only. Tables show as a destination only while Tables is on.

### Settings

- **Accepting responses**, and **Close on a date**.
- **Accept responses from people without an account**: off by default. It asks first when you turn it on.
- **Limit to one response per person**, and **Collect the responder's email**.
- **After someone answers**: **Show a message** or **Send them to a link**, and **Show a Submit another response link**.
- **Notifications**: **Tell these people about each new response**.
- **Closed message**: what people see once the form stops accepting responses.

### Share a form

**Copy link** copies the form's link. A form shared with its **Public link** on can be opened and read by anyone with the link; only people in your workspace can send answers, after they sign in, unless **Accept responses from people without an account** is on. While the public link is on, the arrow beside **Copy link** also gives you the embed code.

### Responses

The **Responses** tab lists every response, with a count. Open one to read it in full. The **...** menu has **Export as CSV** and **Delete all responses**, which asks you to type the form's name first.
