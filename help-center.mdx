Most WorkwrK questions turn out to be one of three things: something was not shared with you, something needs a role you do not have, or a flow has a step nobody mentioned. The pages below cover the real behaviour of the product, checked against what the app actually does.

## How to get help

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check whether it is a sharing rule" icon="eye">
    A surprising share of "it's broken" is really "it was not shared with you". [Why can't I see this?](/help-center/faq/permissions-and-visibility) explains how sharing, roles and the reporting line decide what you see.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your workspace's Owner or Admin" icon="user-cog">
    Roles, tiers, reporting lines, sharing and invitations are all set inside your own workspace, and your Owners and Admins can change any of it without leaving the app. If you are not sure who they are, ask your manager. They find the controls in **Workspace settings**, in the avatar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Escalate account or billing questions" icon="mail">
    Anything your admin cannot fix from inside the workspace, such as a billing or account question, goes through the contact page at `https://workwrk.com/contact`, or billing@workwrk.com for billing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
  There is no in-app support chat and no ticket queue inside WorkwrK. Your workspace's Owners and Admins are the first line for everything to do with access, membership and sharing.
</Callout>
